How to interpret the result

All India Quota (AIQ): MCC conducts counselling for 15% AIQ seats in government medical colleges and specified central institutions, along with seats in participating institutes such as AIIMS and JIPMER as per the applicable rules.

State quota: State counselling authorities manage state quota seats. Eligibility, domicile, reservation, service rules and seat distribution differ by state.

Deemed universities: MCC also conducts counselling for deemed university seats. Fee structures and eligibility conditions can be very different from government colleges.

Frequently asked questions

Is the NEET College Predictor 2027 result guaranteed?

No. It is a planning aid. The actual allotment depends on the official 2027 seat matrix, counselling rules, candidate preferences, category, quota, round and rank movement.

What is the difference between AIQ and state quota?

AIQ counselling is conducted centrally by MCC for applicable All India Quota seats. State authorities conduct counselling for state quota seats and apply their own domicile and reservation rules.

Can I use my NEET marks instead of AIR?

Use your All India Rank for college comparison wherever the published cutoff is expressed as AIR. Marks-to-rank relationships change each year with paper difficulty and candidate performance.

Are the cutoff references for 2025 official?

The references are historical closing-rank summaries based on MCC allotment data. Check the original MCC allotment PDFs for the exact round, seat type and category before relying on a figure.

Does this tool include every medical college in India?

No. The sample table is limited and is provided to explain how to read historical cutoffs. It is not a complete college database or a complete list of available seats.

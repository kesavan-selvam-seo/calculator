NEET UG marking scheme

The latest available NTA NEET UG 2026 bulletin specifies 180 compulsory questions and a maximum of 720 marks. Each correct response earns four marks, an incorrect response loses one mark, and an unanswered question receives zero.

How to calculate your NEET score

Use the answer key or your recorded responses to count correct and incorrect answers. Apply the same calculation to each subject, then add the subject scores to estimate your total raw marks.

  1. Count the correct answers in Physics, Chemistry, Botany and Zoology.
  2. Count the incorrect answers in each subject.
  3. Multiply each correct-answer count by 4.
  4. Subtract the number of incorrect answers from that result.
  5. Add the four subject scores to get your estimated total out of 720.

Raw marks vs NEET percentile and rank

This calculator gives an estimated raw score, not your NEET percentile or All India Rank. Percentile and rank are determined through the official result process and depend on the performance of candidates, applicable tie-breaking rules and NTA's evaluation. Do not treat a raw-score estimate as a guaranteed admission outcome or college prediction.

Final marks can also differ if the official answer key is revised or NTA applies special evaluation rules to a question. Always use the official scorecard as the final result.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my NEET UG marks?

Multiply the number of correct answers by 4 and subtract the number of incorrect answers. Unanswered questions receive zero marks. Add the subject-wise scores to get your estimated total.

What is the maximum mark in NEET UG?

As per the NEET UG 2026 bulletin, the paper has 180 compulsory questions and a maximum of 720 marks: 180 each in Physics and Chemistry and 360 in Biology.

Is there negative marking in NEET?

Yes. Under the 2026 marking scheme, each incorrect answer receives minus one mark. An unanswered question receives zero.

Do I need to enter unanswered questions?

No. Enter only correct and incorrect answers. The tool automatically calculates unanswered questions using the subject limit of 45.

Can I calculate Botany and Zoology marks separately?

Yes. Enter Botany and Zoology answers separately. Each has a 45-question limit in this calculator, and their scores are combined as Biology in your total.

Does this tool predict NEET percentile or All India Rank?

No. It calculates raw marks only. Percentile and rank are released through the official NTA result process.

Is this NEET 2027 calculator based on the confirmed 2027 pattern?

No. The NEET UG 2027 Information Bulletin has not been released yet. This tool currently follows the latest available 2026 pattern and should be checked against the 2027 bulletin when NTA publishes it.

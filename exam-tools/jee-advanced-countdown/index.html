Who can appear for JEE Advanced 2027?

JEE Advanced eligibility is defined separately for each examination year. Candidates generally need to qualify through JEE Main and satisfy the applicable age, number-of-attempts, Class 12 appearance and other conditions. However, the exact 2027 rules must be checked in the official 2027 information brochure once it is released.

Before applying, verify these points in the 2027 brochure:
  • JEE Main qualification and category-wise shortlisting criteria
  • Age limit and date-of-birth conditions
  • Number of permitted attempts
  • Class 12 appearance year and subject requirements
  • Documents and category certificates, where applicable

How to prepare while waiting for the JEE Advanced date

  1. Complete the JEE Advanced syllabus and identify topics that need revision.
  2. Practise previous-year Paper 1 and Paper 2 questions under timed conditions.
  3. Take full-length mock tests and review mistakes after each test.
  4. Build accuracy and time management across Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
  5. Check the official website regularly for the 2027 brochure, registration and exam notices.

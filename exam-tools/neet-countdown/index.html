Who should prepare for NEET UG?

NEET UG is the national entrance examination used for admission to undergraduate medical courses covered by the applicable admission rules. Aspirants planning for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and other programmes that use NEET scores should check the relevant counselling and admission requirements for their target course.

The NEET UG 2027 information bulletin will specify the applicable eligibility rules, including qualifying subjects, qualifying examination, age-related provisions and other conditions. Always use the 2027 bulletin as the final reference before applying.

How to plan your NEET UG 2027 preparation

  1. Build a subject-wise plan for Physics, Chemistry and Biology using the applicable syllabus.
  2. Prioritise NCERT concepts and revise important chapters regularly.
  3. Practise topic-wise questions before moving to full-length mock tests.
  4. Analyse every mock test to identify accuracy, speed and conceptual gaps.
  5. Practise the announced exam interface and procedure once NTA confirms the 2027 mode.
  6. Track official notices for the application, admit card, exam schedule and result.

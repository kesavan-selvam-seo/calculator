Frequently asked questions

When will JEE Main 2027 Session 1 be conducted?

According to the NTA JEE Main 2027 Information Bulletin, Session 1 examinations are scheduled for 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027.

What are the JEE Main 2027 Session 2 exam dates?

The NTA JEE Main 2027 Information Bulletin lists 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 April 2027 for Session 2.

What are the JEE Main 2027 shift timings?

For three-hour papers, Shift 1 is 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon IST and Shift 2 is 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM IST. For the 3.5-hour B.Arch and B.Planning combined paper, the shifts end at 12:30 PM and 6:30 PM respectively. Follow the timings on your admit card.

Where can I check the official JEE Main 2027 schedule?

Check the official NTA JEE Main website and the 2027 Information Bulletin for the latest schedule, notices and candidate instructions.

Does the countdown show my individual exam date and shift?

No. This page counts down to the first scheduled date of each session. Your individual date, paper and shift will be specified by NTA through the candidate login and admit card.

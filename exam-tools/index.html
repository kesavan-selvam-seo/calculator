Frequently asked questions

How can I calculate my NEET marks?

Enter correct and incorrect answers. The calculator applies +4 for correct, −1 for incorrect and zero for unanswered questions.

Are JEE Main 2027 dates confirmed?

Dates marked expected are indicative. Check the official NTA website and information bulletin for confirmed dates and revisions.

Does the syllabus tracker save my progress?

Yes. Progress is stored locally in the browser on your device.

Does the marks calculator predict percentile or rank?

No. It calculates estimated raw marks only, not percentile or rank.

How to calculate JEE Main raw score

Raw score = (Correct answers × 4) − (Incorrect answers × 1)

Unanswered questions carry zero marks, so they do not add to or subtract from your score.

Example: If you answer 18 questions correctly, 5 incorrectly and leave 2 unanswered in a subject:

18 × 4 − 5 = 67 marks

Repeat this calculation for Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, then add the three subject scores to get your Paper 1 raw score.

Raw marks vs NTA percentile: understand the difference

The score calculated here is your raw mark total based on the responses you enter. NTA percentile is a normalised score calculated using the performance of candidates in different shifts. It is not the same as percentage of marks.

Your final percentile and rank can be known only through the official NTA result. Do not use a raw score calculator as a guaranteed percentile or rank predictor.

Frequently asked questions

How is the JEE Main 2027 score calculated?

For JEE Main 2027 Paper 1, each correct answer carries 4 marks, each incorrect answer carries minus 1 mark, and an unanswered question carries 0 marks. Raw score = (4 × correct answers) − incorrect answers.

Is there negative marking for numerical questions in JEE Main 2027?

Yes. The JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 marking scheme applies minus 1 mark for an incorrect answer in both MCQ and numerical-value questions.

What is the total mark of JEE Main 2027 Paper 1?

JEE Main 2027 Paper 1 for B.E. and B.Tech has 75 compulsory questions and a maximum of 300 marks, with 25 questions each in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry.

Does this calculator predict JEE Main percentile or rank?

No. This tool calculates an estimated raw score from the answers entered. NTA percentile scores are calculated separately using the normalisation procedure across shifts, and rank depends on the official result.

Can my JEE Main raw score change after the final answer key?

Yes. Changes to the provisional answer key, dropped questions or NTA's final evaluation rules may affect the final score. Use the official result as the final reference.

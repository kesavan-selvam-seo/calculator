Why NEET marks versus rank changes every year

NEET All India Rank depends on how every candidate performs in the same examination cycle. A score that secured one rank in a previous year may correspond to a different rank in another year because the paper difficulty, candidate performance and score distribution change.

The 2026 result followed a re-examination held on 21 June after the earlier examination was cancelled. Its score distribution should therefore be treated cautiously when estimating 2027 ranks. The official NTA result gives each candidate an AIR; it does not provide a universal marks-to-rank conversion chart.

How to use this NEET rank predictor

  1. Calculate your expected raw score using the final or provisional answer key.
  2. Enter the score in the predictor above.
  3. Review the indicative rank range generated from the 2026 NTA score bands.
  4. Compare it with current-year official results and counselling information when available.

For the most accurate result, wait for the official NTA scorecard. Use the predictor for broad planning only, not for deciding whether to accept or reject a seat or counselling option.

NEET rank, qualifying percentile and admission are different

Qualifying percentile is an eligibility threshold, not your All India Rank. AIR is the overall position assigned by NTA. Admission depends on counselling, seat availability, category, domicile, quota, college preferences and the applicable admission rules. Clearing the qualifying percentile does not guarantee an MBBS seat.

NTA declares the result and AIR; MCC and State counselling authorities conduct their respective counselling processes. Check the official counselling portal for current seat matrices and eligibility requirements.

Frequently asked questions

How can I predict my NEET UG 2027 rank?

Enter your expected marks out of 720. The tool uses selected official NTA NEET 2026 score-distribution points to estimate a broad rank range. It is only a planning estimate, not an official 2027 prediction.

Does NTA publish an official NEET marks vs rank table?

NTA publishes individual scorecards containing All India Rank and selected aggregate score-distribution data. It does not publish a complete official marks-to-rank conversion chart.

Why can the same marks get a different NEET rank every year?

Rank depends on how all candidates perform in that year's examination. Paper difficulty, score distribution, candidate participation, answer-key changes and tie-breaking rules can affect the rank for a given score.

Which year's data does this predictor use?

It uses selected official NTA NEET UG 2026 score-distribution points. The 2026 examination involved a re-examination, so its distribution may not represent NEET 2027.

Can I use this predictor for marks below 500?

This version does not provide an estimate below 500 because the selected official NTA score-band reference points begin at 500. It avoids presenting unsupported low-score rank estimates.

Does this tool predict category rank or MBBS college?

No. It estimates overall AIR only. Category rank, state merit position and college admission depend on official results and counselling rules.

What is the difference between NEET qualifying percentile and All India Rank?

Qualifying percentile determines whether a candidate meets the minimum eligibility threshold. All India Rank is the candidate's position in the overall merit list. Qualifying does not guarantee a seat.

How the Revenue Growth Rate Calculator Works

Formula

Growth rate = (new − old) ÷ old × 100%, when the old value is non-zero.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the required values above.
  2. Check that inputs use the intended units or date format.
  3. Select Calculate to see the result.
  4. Change the inputs to compare another scenario.

Understanding the result

Calculate percentage revenue growth or decline between two periods. The output is calculated using your inputs and the stated formula. Results depend on the selected measurement conventions and assumptions.

Limitations

This tool does not verify input data or automatically account for public holidays, daylight-saving rules or other factors not represented by the formula. Treat business metrics as estimates and keep definitions consistent.

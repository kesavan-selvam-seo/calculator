How sales tax is estimated

For a single tax rate, estimated tax is the taxable purchase amount multiplied by the tax rate as a decimal. Add the tax to the taxable price to estimate the total payable.

Actual treatment depends on jurisdiction, product category, exemptions, local rates, and whether shipping or other fees are taxable. Check the applicable tax authority for the rules that apply to the purchase.

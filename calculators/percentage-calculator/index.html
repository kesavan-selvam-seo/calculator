What is a percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a value out of 100. The symbol % means “per hundred”, so 25% is the same as 25 out of 100, 0.25 as a decimal, or 1/4 as a fraction. Percentages are useful because they put different values on the same scale, making comparisons easier.

You will see percentages in exam marks, discounts, GST and tax, interest rates, salary hikes, business growth, tips, statistics, profit margins and many everyday calculations.

Percentage formulas

1. What is X% of Y?

Result = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Example: 20% of 150 = (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30.

2. X is what percent of Y?

Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 45 out of 180 = 25%.

3. Percentage change

% change = ((new − old) ÷ |old|) × 100

Example: 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.

4. Increase or decrease by X%

New value = original × (1 ± X ÷ 100)

Example: 120 increased by 15% = 138.

5. Reverse percentage

Original = final ÷ (1 ± X ÷ 100)

Example: 92 after a 20% decrease came from 115.

Percent vs percentage points

Percentage points = new % − old %

Moving from 4% to 6% is +2 percentage points, but a 50% relative increase.

Common percentage mistakes

  • Using the wrong base: percentage change is measured against the original value, not the new value.
  • Confusing percent and percentage points: a move from 20% to 25% is +5 percentage points but a 25% relative increase.
  • Assuming equal increases and decreases cancel: +20% and then −20% produces 96 from an original 100.
  • Dividing by zero: “X is what percent of Y?” and percentage change are undefined when the denominator is zero.
  • Rounding too early: keep extra decimals during the calculation and round the final answer when needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 25% of 80 = (25 × 80) ÷ 100 = 20.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 30 out of 150 = (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the absolute original value and multiply by 100. A positive answer indicates an increase; a negative answer indicates a decrease.

How do I increase a number by 15%?

Multiply the number by 1.15. For example, 200 increased by 15% is 200 × 1.15 = 230.

How do I reverse a percentage?

If a final number is after a 20% decrease, divide the final value by 0.80. If it is after a 20% increase, divide by 1.20.

What is the difference between percent change and percentage points?

Percentage points compare two percentage values directly. Percent change measures the relative change from the starting percentage. A move from 4% to 6% is +2 percentage points and +50% relative change.

Are the values I enter stored?

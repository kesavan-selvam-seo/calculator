Understand Price Inflation Impact

Calculate how annual price adjustments diminish currency purchasing capabilities or require higher monetary figures to represent equal values.

How inflation affects purchasing power

A simplified inflation estimate grows a price by the assumed annual inflation rate over a selected period. Projected future cost = current cost × (1 + annual rate)^years.

Actual inflation differs by product, location, and year. Treat the result as a scenario, not a forecast; it does not account for quality changes, personal spending patterns, taxes, or investment returns.

What is CPI?

Consumer Price Index measures the average change over time in the prices paid by urban consumers for a market basket of consumer goods and services.

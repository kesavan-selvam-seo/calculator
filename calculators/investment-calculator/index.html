Understand an investment projection

An investment projection estimates how an initial amount and recurring contributions may grow at the return rate and over the period entered. The estimate depends on contribution timing, compounding frequency, and the assumed rate.

Returns are not guaranteed. The result may not reflect fees, taxes, inflation, market volatility, or losses. Compare scenarios rather than treating a projected value as a promise.

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal, which also includes all of the accumulated interest from previous periods.

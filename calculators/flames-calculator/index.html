FLAMES Calculator Online Guide

What is a FLAMES Calculator?

A FLAMES Calculator is a fun online tool based on the classic childhood FLAMES game. It checks the relationship-style result between two names and gives one result from Friendship, Love, Affection, Marriage, Enemy, or Sibling.

The word FLAMES is made from six result categories. F stands for Friendship, L stands for Love, A stands for Affection, M stands for Marriage, E stands for Enemy, and S stands for Sibling. This calculator makes the classic game quick and easy to use online.

How Does the FLAMES Game Work?

The classic FLAMES game removes common letters from two names and counts the remaining letters. That count is then used to remove letters from the word FLAMES until one final letter remains.

This calculator follows the same playful idea in an online format. It cleans the names, compares letters, calculates the remaining count, and then gives the final FLAMES result instantly.

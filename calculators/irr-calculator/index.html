Understand Investment Returns

The IRR represents the break-even discount rate where the Net Present Value (NPV) of all cash inflows and outflows equals zero.

What internal rate of return means

Internal rate of return (IRR) is the discount rate at which the net present value of a cash-flow series equals zero. It can help compare projects when initial outflows and later cash flows are mapped over time.

Some cash-flow patterns can produce multiple IRRs or no meaningful IRR. Consider net present value, project risk, cash-flow timing, and your assumptions when interpreting a result.

How to use the zodiac sign calculator

  1. Select your birth month.
  2. Select your birth day and click “Find my zodiac sign”.
  3. Read your estimated sun sign, date range, element and modality.

For example, April 10 maps to Aries and September 10 maps to Virgo. The tool compares your month and day with a fixed date table. It does not calculate the Sun’s position for your birth year.

Born near the start or end of a sign?

The Sun’s transition between signs happens at a specific moment and can vary by year. If your birthday is near a boundary, a calendar lookup may differ from your exact sun sign. Use your full birth date, time and time zone for confirmation. You have one sun sign at a given moment; a “cusp” is a boundary, not an additional sign.

Understanding your result

Sun sign: In Western tropical astrology, this is the sign occupied by the Sun at birth. This tool estimates it from familiar calendar ranges.

Element: Fire, Earth, Air and Water are traditional astrological groupings, not scientific personality categories.

Modality: Cardinal, Fixed and Mutable are traditional groupings associated with the beginning, middle and end of a season.

Western zodiac sign vs. Rasi and rising sign

A Western sun sign, a Vedic moon sign (Rasi) and an ascendant (Lagna or rising sign) describe different parts of an astrological chart. They can be different for the same person. This calculator does not determine your moon sign, nakshatra or ascendant.

Method reference: Western zodiac calendar and approximate date ranges.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my zodiac sign by date of birth?

Select your birth month and day, then choose Find my zodiac sign. The calculator matches your birthday to the conventional Western sun-sign date ranges shown below.

Is this my Rasi or Vedic moon sign?

No. This tool estimates a Western tropical sun sign. Rasi usually refers to the Vedic moon sign in Indian birth-chart contexts. A moon-sign calculation needs a different method and can require birth time and location.

What if my birthday is near a zodiac sign change?

Calendar ranges are approximate. The Sun changes signs at a specific moment that varies by year. Near a boundary, use your birth year, exact time and time zone with an astronomical birth-chart calculation to confirm your sun sign.

Do I need my birth year or birth time?

Not for this simple date-range lookup. It uses only month and day. A precise sun sign near a boundary requires more birth details; this tool does not perform that calculation.

What zodiac sign is February 29?

February 29 falls in Pisces in this conventional date table. You can select it directly; no birth year is needed for the lookup.

Does this calculator predict my future?

No. It returns a conventional sun sign and its traditional element and modality. Astrology is not a scientifically validated way to predict personality or future events.

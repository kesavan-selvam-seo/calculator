How electricity cost is estimated

For an appliance rated in watts, energy used in kilowatt-hours is estimated as watts × hours used ÷ 1,000. Multiply kWh by the tariff per kWh to estimate the usage charge.

The result may exclude fixed service fees, fuel adjustments, taxes, tiered tariffs, and time-of-use pricing. Use the rate and period shown on your bill for a closer estimate.

What is a kWh?

It stands for Kilowatt-hour, the standard unit of energy representing 1000 Watts consumed for 1 hour.

Fuel Cost Calculator Guides

Calculate Trip Fuel Budgets

Planning a road trip? Estimate gasoline or diesel costs based on vehicle mileage ratings and local pump rates.

Fuel cost formulas and unit assumptions

For km/L, estimated litres = distance in kilometres ÷ km/L. For L/100 km, estimated litres = distance in kilometres × L/100 km ÷ 100. For US MPG, estimated US gallons = distance in miles ÷ MPG; one US gallon is approximately 3.78541 litres.

Choose distance, efficiency, and fuel-price units that match your trip and pump price. The estimate does not account for traffic, driving style, route changes, fuel-price variation, or differences between advertised and real-world economy.

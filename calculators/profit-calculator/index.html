Profit, margin, and markup

Profit is revenue minus total cost. Profit margin expresses profit as a percentage of revenue, while markup expresses profit as a percentage of cost; these percentages answer different questions and are not interchangeable.

Use consistent treatment of unit cost, overheads, discounts, and taxes. The estimate includes only the amounts entered and may not represent net accounting profit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit divided by sales price. Markup is profit divided by cost price.

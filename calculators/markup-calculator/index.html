How the Markup Calculator Works

Formula

Markup = cost × rate ÷ 100; selling price = cost + markup.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your values in the fields above.
  2. Confirm that your inputs use the correct units.
  3. Select Calculate to view the result.
  4. Change an input to compare scenarios.

Understanding the result

Calculate a markup amount and selling price from cost and markup percentage. The output uses the stated formula and your inputs. Results may vary with unit conventions and rounding.

Limitations

This calculator does not independently verify input data or account for factors that are not represented by its formula. Business calculations are estimates and should be interpreted with the assumptions in mind.

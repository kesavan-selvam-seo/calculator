How the Time Zone Offset Converter Works

Formula

Understanding the result

Convert a local date and time between two manually entered UTC offsets. The output is calculated using your inputs and the stated formula. Results depend on the selected measurement conventions and assumptions.

Limitations

This tool does not verify input data or automatically account for public holidays, daylight-saving rules or other factors not represented by the formula. Treat business metrics as estimates and keep definitions consistent.

<h2 id="national">Important National Days in December 2026 in India</h2>
<h3>December 2 – National Pollution Control Day</h3>
<p>National Pollution Control Day is observed in India on December 2 in memory of the people who lost their lives in the 1984 Bhopal gas tragedy. The day highlights the importance of industrial safety, pollution prevention and responsible environmental practices.</p>
<h3>December 4 – Indian Navy Day</h3>
<p>Indian Navy Day recognises the role and achievements of the Indian Navy. The date commemorates Operation Trident, the Indian Navy’s attack on Karachi harbour during the 1971 Indo-Pakistani War.</p>
<h3>December 6 – Mahaparinirvan Diwas</h3>
<p>Mahaparinirvan Diwas marks the death anniversary of Dr B. R. Ambedkar, the principal architect of the Indian Constitution and a leading advocate of social justice and equality. People pay tribute to his contribution to modern India.</p>
<h3>December 7 – Armed Forces Flag Day</h3>
<p>Armed Forces Flag Day honours the service and sacrifice of personnel from the Indian Army, Navy and Air Force. It also encourages citizens to contribute to the welfare and rehabilitation of serving and retired armed forces personnel and their families.</p>
<h3>December 14 – National Energy Conservation Day</h3>
<p>Observed by the Ministry of Power, National Energy Conservation Day promotes efficient energy use and encourages individuals, industries and institutions to reduce energy waste. The National Energy Conservation Awards are also associated with this observance.</p>
<h3>December 16 – Vijay Diwas</h3>
<p>Vijay Diwas commemorates India’s victory in the 1971 war and the surrender of Pakistani forces in Dhaka on December 16, 1971. The day honours the courage and sacrifice of Indian armed forces personnel.</p>
<h3>December 18 – Minorities Rights Day</h3>
<p>Minorities Rights Day is observed in India to raise awareness about the rights, dignity and protection of religious and linguistic minority communities. It highlights equality, inclusion and constitutional safeguards.</p>
<h3>December 19 – Goa Liberation Day</h3>
<p>Goa Liberation Day marks the end of Portuguese colonial rule in Goa in 1961. It commemorates the liberation of Goa following Operation Vijay.</p>
<h3>December 22 – National Mathematics Day</h3>
<p>India observes National Mathematics Day on the birth anniversary of mathematician Srinivasa Ramanujan. The day celebrates his contributions to number theory, mathematical analysis and infinite series, while encouraging interest in mathematics among students.</p>
<h3>December 23 – National Farmers’ Day (Kisan Diwas)</h3>
<p>Kisan Diwas recognises the contribution of farmers to India’s economy and food security. It is observed on the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Chaudhary Charan Singh, who was known for his work on agricultural and rural issues.</p>
<h3>December 24 – National Consumer Day</h3>
<p>National Consumer Day marks the enactment of the Consumer Protection Act, 1986, on December 24. The observance promotes consumer awareness and the importance of informed choices, fair trade practices and consumer rights.</p>
<h3>December 25 – Good Governance Day</h3>
<p>Good Governance Day is observed on the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee. It promotes transparent, accountable, inclusive and citizen-focused public administration. Christmas is also celebrated on December 25 by Christians around the world.</p>
<h3>December 26 – Veer Baal Diwas</h3>
<p>Veer Baal Diwas honours the courage and sacrifice of the four Sahibzadas, the sons of Guru Gobind Singh. The day encourages people to remember their bravery and commitment to their faith.</p>

<h2 id="international">International and United Nations Days in December 2026</h2>
<h3>December 1 – World AIDS Day</h3>
<p>World AIDS Day raises awareness about HIV, remembers people who have died from AIDS-related illnesses and highlights the importance of prevention, testing, treatment and support for people living with HIV.</p>
<h3>December 2 – International Day for the Abolition of Slavery</h3>
<p>The United Nations observes this day to highlight the need to eliminate contemporary forms of slavery, including human trafficking, forced labour, child labour and forced marriage.</p>
<h3>December 3 – International Day of Persons with Disabilities</h3>
<p>This UN observance promotes the rights, dignity and well-being of persons with disabilities. It also encourages inclusive communities and equal participation in social, economic and public life.</p>
<h3>December 5 – World Soil Day and International Volunteer Day</h3>
<p>World Soil Day draws attention to the importance of healthy soil for food production, ecosystems and climate resilience. International Volunteer Day recognises volunteers and their contribution to sustainable development and communities.</p>
<h3>December 9 – International Anti-Corruption Day</h3>
<p>International Anti-Corruption Day highlights the social and economic harm caused by corruption and encourages action to strengthen transparency, accountability and integrity.</p>
<h3>December 10 – Human Rights Day</h3>
<p>Human Rights Day marks the adoption of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights by the United Nations General Assembly on December 10, 1948. It reinforces the principles of equality, freedom and dignity for all people.</p>
<h3>December 11 – International Mountain Day</h3>
<p>International Mountain Day highlights the importance of mountain ecosystems and the communities that depend on them. It encourages sustainable development and the protection of mountain biodiversity.</p>
<h3>December 12 – Universal Health Coverage Day</h3>
<p>This day advocates access to quality and affordable health services for everyone without financial hardship. It highlights the importance of strong and inclusive health systems.</p>
<h3>December 18 – International Migrants Day</h3>
<p>International Migrants Day recognises the contributions of migrants and draws attention to the need to protect their rights, safety and dignity.</p>
<h3>December 20 – International Human Solidarity Day</h3>
<p>This UN day promotes unity in diversity and encourages international cooperation to address poverty and other global challenges.</p>
<h3>December 21 – World Basketball Day</h3>
<p>World Basketball Day recognises basketball’s role in bringing people and communities together and promoting participation in sport across countries and cultures.</p>
<h3>December 27 – International Day of Epidemic Preparedness</h3>
<p>The United Nations observes International Day of Epidemic Preparedness on December 27. It highlights the importance of prevention, preparedness and international cooperation to respond to epidemics and protect public health.</p>

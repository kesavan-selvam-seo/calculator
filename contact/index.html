A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Help us understand
your request.

A specific example makes it easier to investigate an issue or understand an idea.

What should I include in a calculator report?

Share the calculator URL, the values and units you entered, the result shown, and the result you expected. If you're comparing it with another source, include that reference too.

How do I suggest a correction to an article?

Include the article URL and identify the sentence, date, or detail that needs attention. Explain the proposed correction and, where possible, share a relevant official or original source.

Can I request a new topic or calculator?

Yes. Tell us what you'd like to see, who it would help, and the question it should answer. For a calculator, example inputs or a suggested formula can be useful.

Can you provide personalised professional advice?

CalcuPortal provides general information, learning resources, and estimates. We don't provide personalised medical, financial, tax, or legal advice. Please consult an appropriate qualified professional for decisions specific to your circumstances.

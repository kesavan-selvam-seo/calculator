UPS Full Form – Uninterruptible Power Supply

The full form of UPS is Uninterruptible Power Supply. It is used in technology & computer and refers to a device that provides backup power when the main supply fails.

UPS stands for Uninterruptible Power Supply.
A device that provides backup power when the main supply fails.

What Does UPS Mean?

A UPS supplies temporary power from a battery when mains electricity fails or fluctuates. Backup time depends on battery capacity, battery condition and the load connected.

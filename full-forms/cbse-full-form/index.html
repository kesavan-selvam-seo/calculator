What Does CBSE Mean?

The Central Board of Secondary Education is an Indian national-level school education board. It affiliates schools, prescribes curriculum frameworks and conducts board examinations for eligible classes. Students and parents commonly encounter CBSE in relation to syllabi, academic resources, assessment and school affiliation.

CBC Full Form – Complete Blood Count

The full form of CBC is Complete Blood Count. It is used in medical and refers to a blood test that measures several components of blood, including red cells, white cells and platelets.

What Does CBC Mean?

A complete blood count measures several components of blood, commonly including red blood cells, white blood cells, haemoglobin, haematocrit and platelets. Clinicians use the results as one part of assessing general health and investigating possible conditions. Results need interpretation alongside symptoms and other tests.

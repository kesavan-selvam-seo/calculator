ECG Full Form – Electrocardiogram

The full form of ECG is Electrocardiogram. It is used in medical and refers to a test that records the electrical activity of the heart.

ECG stands for Electrocardiogram.

What Does ECG Mean?

An ECG uses skin electrodes to record the heart's electrical activity. Clinicians assess the tracing alongside symptoms and medical history; it is one part of a wider evaluation.

What Is the Full Form of an Abbreviation?

A full form is the complete version of a shortened term. An abbreviation reduces a longer word or phrase, while an acronym is formed from the initial letters of a phrase and is usually pronounced as a word. For example, NASA stands for National Aeronautics and Space Administration, while HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language.

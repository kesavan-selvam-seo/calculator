What is a full form?

A full form is the expanded version of an abbreviation, acronym or initialism. For example, HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. Full forms are commonly used in education, computing, communication, science and business to make long names easier to write and remember.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between an abbreviation and an acronym?

An abbreviation is a shortened form of a word or phrase. An acronym is formed from initial letters and is usually pronounced as a word; an initialism is pronounced letter by letter.

Can one abbreviation have multiple full forms?

Yes. The same letters can represent different terms in different subjects or industries. Always use the surrounding context to identify the correct meaning.

What Is the Full Form of an Abbreviation?

A full form is the complete version of a shortened term. An abbreviation reduces a longer word or phrase, while an acronym is formed from the initial letters of a phrase and is usually pronounced as a word. For example, NASA stands for National Aeronautics and Space Administration, while HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. An abbreviation is a shortened form of a word or phrase. An acronym is formed from initial letters and is usually pronounced as a word; an initialism is pronounced letter by letter.

Can one abbreviation have multiple full forms?

Yes. The same letters can represent different terms in different subjects or industries. Always use the surrounding context to identify the correct meaning.

How do I search for a full form on this page?

Type the abbreviation or a related word into the search box. The list filters as you search, and you can narrow it further using the topic buttons.

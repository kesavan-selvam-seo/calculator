SIM Full Form – Subscriber Identity Module

The full form of SIM is Subscriber Identity Module. It is used in general knowledge and refers to a chip that identifies a subscriber on a mobile network.

SIM stands for Subscriber Identity Module.
A chip that identifies a subscriber on a mobile network.

What Does SIM Mean?

A SIM stores subscriber information and credentials used by a mobile network to identify and authenticate a subscription. It may be a removable card or an embedded eSIM profile.

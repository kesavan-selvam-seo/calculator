02

Understanding calculator results

A calculator’s output depends on your inputs and the formula, units, assumptions, and rounding used by that tool. Check the instructions and make sure the values you enter match the requested units and time period.

Some tools perform straightforward arithmetic; others estimate real-world outcomes using simplified models. Even a mathematically correct result may not account for every factor relevant to your situation. Results are not official determinations or guarantees of a particular outcome.

Before relying on an important result, review the underlying assumptions and verify it with an appropriate independent source. An example calculation is an illustration, not a prediction about you.

05

Education, mathematics & dates

Educational articles and tools support learning and everyday calculations. They do not provide academic certification or guarantee exam scores, admission, eligibility, or other official outcomes.

Exam dates, application requirements, syllabus details, and eligibility criteria can change. Confirm them with the relevant examination body, institution, or official notification.

For mathematics, conversions, and date calculations, check precision, units, and conventions. For example, counting both the start and end date can produce a different total from counting elapsed days. Verify calculations used for official deadlines, technical work, or other accuracy-critical tasks.

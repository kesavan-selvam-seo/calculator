01

About this policy

CalcuPortal provides online calculators, articles, and practical guides. This policy explains how information is handled when you browse our website, use a calculator, or contact us. “We” and “us” refer to CalcuPortal.

You can read our articles and use our calculators without creating an account. Some technical information is still processed by the services that deliver and measure the website.

02

Your calculator inputs

The current calculator code works out results in your browser, rather than sending your entries to a CalcuPortal calculation server. This includes values entered into our finance, health, mathematics, date, and conversion tools.

This does not mean browsing is anonymous: hosting and analytics services process information about visits, as explained below. If you include calculator inputs in a contact message, those details become part of your enquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive financial, health, or identity information.

03

Information we use

When you contact us

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. We use these details to respond, investigate reported problems, and consider feedback or suggestions. Messages are submitted through Web3Forms and received in our Gmail mailbox. Direct emails also become part of our correspondence.

When you browse

CalcuPortal uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and improve content and navigation. Our website sends page information, including the page title and URL, to Google Analytics. Google may also process browser, device, interaction, and approximate location information through its analytics service.

Our hosting and external content providers receive technical information needed to deliver requests, such as your IP address and browser information. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

04

Cookies & browser storage

Our theme setting is saved in your browser’s local storage so your light or dark preference can be remembered. It remains there until you change it or clear the site’s stored data.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits. Our current site loads Google Analytics when a page opens and does not provide an on-site cookie preference centre.

You can block or clear cookies and site storage in your browser settings. Clearing local storage resets your theme preference; it does not delete messages you have sent or provider logs. You can also explore Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Browser controls and extensions vary in what they block.

06

Advertising

The current website implementation does not load advertising tags. If we introduce advertising, we will update this policy to describe the providers and relevant data practices.

If Google advertising is introduced, Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on previous visits to this and other websites. Google provides personalised advertising controls through Ads Settings. This describes a possible future advertising service, not an advertising feature currently active on CalcuPortal.

07

Retention & security

Contact correspondence may remain in our mailbox and form provider’s systems. Retention depends on the enquiry, any continuing support or security needs, and provider settings and policies. We do not publish a fixed retention period for all messages or provider logs. You can contact us to request deletion of information associated with your enquiry.

The website uses HTTPS to protect information in transit. No website, email system, or storage service can guarantee complete security. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or confidential records through the contact form.

08

International processing

Our service providers operate internationally, so information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Their privacy policies explain the locations, safeguards, and rights that apply to their services.

09

Your choices & requests

You can browse without contacting us, limit what you include in a message, and use the browser and Google controls linked above.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email infocalcuportal@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. Describe the request and, where relevant, the email address used to contact us. We may need enough information to verify your request. Some records may need to be retained where applicable law or legitimate security requirements allow or require it.

10

Children’s privacy

Our calculators and articles are general information resources. We do not ask children to submit personal or sensitive information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the message and address an appropriate deletion request.

11

External links & policy updates

Our articles and pages may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, so review them before sharing information.

We may update this policy when our website, services, or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. This policy explains our practices; simply browsing this page is not a substitute for consent where consent is required.

Contact us about privacy

For privacy questions or requests about your information, contact CalcuPortal at infocalcuportal@gmail.com.

For general feedback, calculator issues, or article suggestions, visit our Contact page.

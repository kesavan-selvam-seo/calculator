T20 World Cup Winners List

Updated October 3, 2026

The T20 World Cup winners list covers the champions of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup from the inaugural tournament in 2007 through the latest completed edition in 2026. India are the current champions and the first team to win the men's T20 World Cup three times, after defeating New Zealand in the 2026 final.

Here is the complete year-wise list of ICC Men's T20 World Cup winners, runners-up, hosts and final results. The competition began in 2007 with 12 teams and expanded to 20 teams by the 2024 edition.

Latest T20 World Cup winner: India won the 2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup by defeating New Zealand by 96 runs in Ahmedabad. India scored 255/5 and restricted New Zealand to 159, becoming the first team to win back-to-back Men's T20 World Cup titles and the first to win three titles.

T20 World Cup Winners by Year

2007 – India

India won the inaugural Men's T20 World Cup in South Africa under MS Dhoni. They defeated Pakistan by five runs in a dramatic final at Johannesburg. The tournament also featured Yuvraj Singh's six sixes in an over against England.

2009 – Pakistan

Pakistan won their first T20 World Cup title in England in 2009. Shahid Afridi was central to the final, scoring an unbeaten 54 as Pakistan defeated Sri Lanka by eight wickets at Lord's.

2010 – England

England became the first men's team to win an ICC global white-ball title when they defeated Australia by seven wickets in the 2010 final in Barbados. Kevin Pietersen was named Player of the Tournament.

2012 – West Indies

West Indies won their first Men's T20 World Cup in Sri Lanka in 2012. They defeated the hosts by 36 runs in the final, with Marlon Samuels making 78 and Sunil Narine taking three wickets.

2014 – Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka claimed their first T20 World Cup title in Bangladesh in 2014 by defeating India by six wickets. Kumar Sangakkara finished the chase with an unbeaten half-century in what became a memorable farewell tournament for Sangakkara and Mahela Jayawardene.

2016 – West Indies

West Indies became the first team to win the Men's T20 World Cup twice when they defeated England by four wickets in the final at Eden Gardens. Carlos Brathwaite hit four consecutive sixes from the last over to complete the chase.

2021 – Australia

Australia won their first Men's T20 World Cup title in 2021 after defeating New Zealand by eight wickets in Dubai. Mitchell Marsh scored an unbeaten 77 and was named Player of the Match.

2022 – England

England won their second T20 World Cup in Australia in 2022, defeating Pakistan by five wickets at the Melbourne Cricket Ground. Sam Curran was named Player of the Match and Player of the Tournament. England also became the first team to hold the men's ODI and T20 World Cup titles simultaneously.

2024 – India

India won their second Men's T20 World Cup in 2024, defeating South Africa by seven runs in the final at Kensington Oval in Barbados. Virat Kohli scored 76 and was named Player of the Match, while Jasprit Bumrah played a decisive role with the ball. India joined England and West Indies as the third team to win the tournament twice.

2026 – India

India became the first three-time Men's T20 World Cup champions in 2026. They defeated New Zealand by 96 runs in Ahmedabad after scoring 255/5, the highest total in a T20 World Cup final. India also became the first team to defend the T20 World Cup title and the first to win the men's tournament on home soil.

How Many T20 World Cups Has India Won?

India have won the Men's T20 World Cup three times: 2007, 2024 and 2026. Their 2026 victory made them the first team to win three titles and the first to win consecutive editions.

How Many T20 World Cups Has West Indies Won?

West Indies have won the Men's T20 World Cup twice, in 2012 and 2016. They were the first team to win the men's competition more than once. Their 2016 final victory over England came after Carlos Brathwaite hit four consecutive sixes in the final over.

How Many T20 World Cups Has England Won?

England have won the Men's T20 World Cup twice, in 2010 and 2022. Their 2022 title came against Pakistan in Melbourne, with Sam Curran taking Player of the Match and Player of the Tournament honours.

T20 World Cup Winners: Important Records

  • Most titles: India – 3.
  • First champions: India in 2007.
  • Most recent champions: India in 2026.
  • First team to win consecutive men's T20 World Cups: India, in 2024 and 2026.
  • First team to win three men's T20 World Cups: India, in 2026.
  • First team to win on home soil: India in 2026.
  • Largest winning margin in a Men's T20 World Cup final by runs: India beat New Zealand by 96 runs in 2026.
  • Highest total in a T20 World Cup final: India's 255/5 against New Zealand in 2026.
  • Most teams in an edition: 20 teams, starting in 2024.

These records reflect the tournament through the completed 2026 edition and should be updated after future editions.

Who Was Player of the Tournament in the 2026 T20 World Cup?

India opener Sanju Samson was named the Player of the Tournament at the 2026 Men's T20 World Cup. He scored 321 runs in five innings at an average of 80.25 and a strike rate of 199.37. He made 89 in the final against New Zealand after scoring 89 in the semi-final against England.

When Is the Next T20 World Cup?

The next ICC Men's T20 World Cup is scheduled for 2028 in Australia and New Zealand. The ICC has confirmed that the event will remain a 20-team tournament, with a revised format beginning in 2028.

Men's T20 World Cup vs T20 World Cup

This page covers the ICC Men's T20 World Cup. The women's tournament is a separate ICC competition with its own winners, editions and records. Using "Men's" in the title and content helps avoid mixing the two competitions when comparing historical winners.

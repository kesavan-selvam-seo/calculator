Fastest Centuries in IPL History

Updated October 17, 2026

The fastest IPL century is measured by the number of legal deliveries a batter needs to reach 100 runs. The record belongs to Chris Gayle, who reached his hundred in 30 balls for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Pune Warriors India in 2013.

Gayle's 30-Ball Record

Gayle's 175 not out remains the highest individual score in IPL history, and his 30-ball hundred is still the fastest century through IPL 2026. The innings combined rapid boundary hitting with a large final score and became one of the league's defining records.

Fastest Indian Century in IPL

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's 35-ball hundred for Rajasthan Royals against Gujarat Titans in 2025 set a major Indian-player milestone for speed to a century. His further 36-ball hundred in 2026 also reinforced how quickly records in this category can move, even when the all-time record remains unchanged.

Fast Hundreds and Match Conditions

Ball count alone does not describe the whole innings. A batter may enter early or as a finisher, face different pitch conditions, or benefit from a partnership that keeps the required run rate low. For the full result, read the match scorecard as well as the century speed.

Frequently Asked Questions

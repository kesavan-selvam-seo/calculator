Most Runs in IPL History: Top Run-Scorers Through 2026

Updated October 17, 2026

The all-time IPL runs list combines a batter's runs across every franchise they have represented. Unlike the Orange Cap, which is awarded to a season's leading scorer, career-run rankings reward consistency and longevity across multiple editions.

Who Holds the All-Time IPL Runs Record?

Virat Kohli topped the list with 9,336 runs through IPL 2026 and became the first player to pass 9,000 IPL runs. He has represented Royal Challengers Bengaluru throughout his IPL career, making his record a rare example of sustained production for one franchise.

Rohit Sharma ranked second with 7,329 runs, followed by Shikhar Dhawan and David Warner. Among the leaders, Kohli and Rohit remain active and can extend their totals in future seasons; Dhawan, Warner, Raina and de Villiers' IPL totals are historical records rather than live totals.

Career Runs vs Orange Cap

A player can top the all-time career list without winning the Orange Cap in a particular season. The Orange Cap measures runs in one edition only. See the IPL Orange Cap winners list for the season award and the champions list for team results.

Single-Season Run Record

Kohli's 973 runs in IPL 2016 remain the highest individual season tally through IPL 2026. Other exceptional seasons include Shubman Gill's 890 runs in 2023 and Jos Buttler's 863 in 2022. A season leaderboard should be treated separately from the career ranking above.

Most Sixes in a Single IPL Season

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi hit 72 sixes for Rajasthan Royals in IPL 2026, setting the single-season record. He scored 776 runs and faced 327 balls, so sixes accounted for a major share of his run total. Before that season, Chris Gayle's 59 sixes for Royal Challengers Bangalore in 2012 had been the benchmark.

Career Record vs Season Record

A career list rewards players who stay in the competition for many seasons. A single-season list highlights an unusually productive campaign and can be reshaped in one edition. Do not compare the two totals as if they represent the same achievement.

IPL Auction and Player-Retention Rules Explained

Updated October 17, 2026

Before an IPL season, each franchise builds its squad through player retentions, releases and an auction conducted under that cycle's regulations. The process helps teams form a competitive roster while following league rules. Retention allowances, purse values and some bidding mechanisms can change between auction cycles.

How an IPL Auction Works

  1. Player registration: Eligible players enter the auction process under the published rules.
  2. Retentions and releases: Before the auction, teams may keep permitted players and release others under that cycle's framework.
  3. Auction pool: Players available for bidding are offered according to the published process.
  4. Bidding: Franchises bid for available players, subject to their remaining purse and squad rules.
  5. Squad completion: Teams fill slots while respecting squad size, overseas-player provisions and other regulations.

Mini Auction vs Mega Auction

A mega auction is designed to allow substantial squad rebuilding, using a specific retention framework announced by the league. A mini auction generally allows teams to fill gaps or make more targeted changes. The exact number of retentions, purse values and any special mechanisms must be checked for each official auction announcement.

What Does Retention Mean?

Retention means a franchise keeps an eligible player before the auction rather than letting that player enter the available pool. A released player may be eligible for auction depending on the regulations. Retention rules can differ by player category and auction cycle, including the treatment of capped and uncapped players.

What Is the IPL Auction Purse?

The purse is the budget a franchise can use under the auction's financial rules. It is not simply a list of individual salaries; retention values, contracts, replacements and league regulations affect how the squad budget works. Purse amounts can change, so an amount from a past auction should not be assumed to apply to the next one.

Overseas Players and Squad Rules

Teams must comply with the overseas-player limit. Under the published player regulations, no more than four overseas players may be named in the starting eleven. Additional roster and replacement rules should be checked in the current season regulations.

Where to Check the Latest Rules

Do not rely on an old purse amount or retention count for a future season. The BCCI and IPL publish official announcements for each auction cycle; consult the official IPL website for confirmed changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an IPL auction?

It is the process where franchises bid for registered players who are available under the season's auction rules.

What is a mega auction?

A mega auction is a squad-rebuilding cycle with a retention framework set by the league.

Is the IPL auction purse the same every year?

No. Purse values and related financial rules may change between auction cycles.

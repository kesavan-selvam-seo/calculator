IPL Impact Player Rule Explained

Updated October 17, 2026

The IPL Impact Player rule allows a team to replace one player during a match with a nominated substitute. Introduced in IPL 2023, it gives teams an extra tactical choice: they may add a batter, bowler or all-rounder depending on the match situation and the applicable playing conditions.

How the Impact Player Rule Works

  • Before the match, a team submits its starting eleven and the permitted substitute list under the season regulations.
  • A team can use one Impact Player in a match; use of the option is not compulsory.
  • The selected substitute replaces a player in the original playing group and participates under the Impact Player provisions.
  • The replaced player cannot resume participation later in the same match, subject to the current rules.
  • The captain and team management choose the timing based on whether extra batting, bowling or balance is needed.

Overseas Player Restriction

The overseas-player limit continues to apply. A team cannot have more than four overseas players on the field at one time. If four overseas players are already in the starting eleven, the Impact Player must be an Indian player. If fewer overseas players start, an overseas Impact Player can only be used when the on-field limit remains satisfied.

Why Teams Use an Impact Player

A team batting first might replace a batter with a specialist bowler for the chase. A team chasing may use an extra batter if it needs more depth. The decision depends on wickets lost, pitch behaviour, the required run rate and match conditions.

Important Limitations

The Impact Player is not a free extra player without restrictions. Eligibility, substitution windows and playing rights are governed by the applicable season's official playing conditions. Those provisions may be revised, so check the current regulations before relying on this explanation for a future edition.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the IPL Impact Player rule introduced?

It was introduced in IPL 2023.

Can an IPL team use more than one Impact Player?

No, a team can use one Impact Player under the current rules.

Can an overseas player be used as the Impact Player?

It depends on how many overseas players are already on the field; the limit of four overseas players at a time still applies.

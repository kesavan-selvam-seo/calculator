Highest Team Totals in IPL History

Updated October 17, 2026

The highest team total in IPL history is 287/3, posted by Sunrisers Hyderabad against Royal Challengers Bengaluru at the M. Chinnaswamy Stadium on 15 April 2024. SRH's innings featured explosive batting at the top and remains the league's benchmark team score through IPL 2026.

Why Have IPL Totals Become Larger?

Modern batting depth, aggressive powerplay tactics, boundary-friendly conditions at some venues and the use of Impact Players have all shaped the scoring environment. The rules, equipment, pitches, and tactical approaches differ from era to era, so raw totals should be read in their season context rather than treated as a direct measure of which team was strongest.

How the 2026 Season Added to the Records

Punjab Kings and Delhi Capitals both produced totals above 260 during 2026, showing that high totals are not limited to one franchise. These recent scores still sit below SRH's 287/3 record, which is a reminder that the all-time record can survive even when multiple teams cross 250 in a season.

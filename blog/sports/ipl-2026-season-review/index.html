IPL 2026 Season Review: Champions, Awards and Records

Updated October 17, 2026

IPL 2026 concluded on 31 May 2026, with Royal Challengers Bengaluru defeating Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad. RCB chased the target and retained the title they won in 2025, becoming the third franchise after Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians to defend an IPL championship successfully.

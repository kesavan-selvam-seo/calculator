IPL History: From the First Season to 2026

Updated October 17, 2026

The Indian Premier League began in 2008 as a professional Twenty20 franchise competition run by the BCCI. It grew from its original team line-up into one of the most followed domestic T20 leagues, with season-long league matches, playoffs, auction cycles and individual awards.

How the IPL Started

The inaugural season was held in 2008. Rajasthan Royals won the first title after defeating Chennai Super Kings in the final. The opening match is also remembered for Brendon McCullum's 158 not out for Kolkata Knight Riders against Royal Challengers Bengaluru (then known as Royal Challengers Bangalore), an innings that helped put the new competition in the spotlight.

How the Tournament Format Evolved

The number of franchises and the league-stage schedule have changed in different periods. Teams have been added, renamed, suspended or replaced, and current franchise names do not always match names used in older scorecards. For comparisons across years, check the season in which a result occurred rather than applying today's team names to every historical row.

The contemporary competition includes a league stage followed by a four-team playoff system. Read the IPL rules and format guide and points system and playoff guide to understand how teams progress to the final.

What to Keep in Mind When Reading IPL History

IPL records are date-sensitive. Career totals can change every season, player names and franchise identities can vary across a career, and auction or playing-condition rules may change.

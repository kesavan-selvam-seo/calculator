IPL Purple Cap Winners List from 2008 to 2026

Updated October 17, 2026

The IPL Purple Cap recognises the bowler who finishes the season with the most wickets. The leaderboard moves as matches are played, so the end-of-season list is the reliable way to compare winners. The table below covers all completed seasons from 2008 through 2026.

IPL Purple Cap Records

Most wickets in a season: Dwayne Bravo (2013) and Harshal Patel (2021) both took 32 wickets in one edition, the joint-highest seasonal tally through IPL 2026.

Multiple winners: Dwayne Bravo, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Harshal Patel and Kagiso Rabada have each won the award twice by the end of IPL 2026.

Latest winner: Kagiso Rabada took 29 wickets for Gujarat Titans in IPL 2026. Season stats should always be dated because the leaderboard can change while the tournament is in progress.

What Is the Difference Between the Purple Cap and Career Wickets?

The Purple Cap is awarded for a single season. It does not necessarily go to the bowler with the most wickets across the player's IPL career. For the career leaderboard, go to most wickets in IPL history. For season batting leaders, see the Orange Cap archive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who won the Purple Cap in IPL 2026?

Kagiso Rabada won the 2026 Purple Cap with 29 wickets for Gujarat Titans.

Who has taken the most wickets in a single IPL season?

Dwayne Bravo and Harshal Patel share the record of 32 wickets in a season, in 2013 and 2021 respectively.

Who has won the Purple Cap more than once?

By the end of IPL 2026, Dwayne Bravo, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Harshal Patel and Kagiso Rabada had each won it twice.

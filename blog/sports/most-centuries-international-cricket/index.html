Most Centuries in International Cricket

Updated October 2026

Sachin Tendulkar holds the men's international record for most centuries, with 100 international hundreds: 51 in Tests and 49 in ODIs. He remains the only men's cricketer to reach 100 international centuries.

Who Has Scored the Most Centuries in International Cricket?

Sachin Tendulkar is the men's all-format leader with 100 international centuries. His total consists of 51 Test centuries and 49 ODI centuries. ICC's Hall of Fame profile records both the 100-century milestone and his career totals.

Virat Kohli's International Centuries

Virat Kohli is the active name closest to Tendulkar's international-century record. ICC reported his 55th ODI century in September 2026, when he scored an unbeaten 139 against West Indies.

Kohli's international-century count should be treated as a live statistic because he continues to play international cricket. For an exact current all-format total, the latest ICC player statistics should be checked before publication.

Most T20I Centuries

T20I century records are separate from Test and ODI records. Because T20I totals can change quickly as active players add matches, use the latest ICC statistics when publishing a live T20I leaderboard.

How Many Centuries Did Sachin Tendulkar Score?

Sachin Tendulkar scored 100 international centuries: 51 in Tests and 49 in ODIs. He did not score a T20I century. His 100th international hundred came against Bangladesh in the 2012 Asia Cup.

Important International Century Records

  • Most men's international centuries: Sachin Tendulkar – 100.
  • Most Test centuries: Sachin Tendulkar – 51.
  • Most ODI centuries: Virat Kohli – 55 as of September 2026.
  • First men's cricketer to score 100 international centuries: Sachin Tendulkar.
  • Tendulkar's 100th century: scored against Bangladesh in Dhaka in March 2012.

International Centuries vs IPL Centuries

International centuries and IPL centuries are different statistical categories. A century scored for India in an international match counts toward a player's international record; a century for an IPL franchise does not.

Men's and Women's International Century Records

Men's and women's international cricket statistics are maintained separately. A women's international-century leaderboard should therefore be compared within the women's record set rather than combined with the men's figures above.

IPL Orange Cap Winners List from 2008 to 2026

Updated October 17, 2026

The IPL Orange Cap is awarded to the batter who finishes the season with the most runs. The leaderboard changes during the tournament, but the end-of-season winner is determined from the completed season's batting records. This table covers every winner through IPL 2026.

IPL Orange Cap Records

Highest season tally: Virat Kohli scored 973 runs in IPL 2016, the competition's single-season record through 2026.

Most Orange Caps: David Warner won the award three times, in 2015, 2017 and 2019. Virat Kohli won it in 2016 and 2024, while Chris Gayle won in consecutive seasons, 2011 and 2012.

Latest winner: Vaibhav Sooryavanshi led the 2026 season with 776 runs for Rajasthan Royals. That total included a season-best strike rate and 72 sixes, making it one of the most explosive Orange Cap-winning seasons.

How the Orange Cap Works

The cap is a season award for the leading run-scorer. During an active IPL season, the cap may be worn by the batter currently leading the run-scoring standings. The final award is based on the completed season leaderboard, not on which player wore it most often.

IPL Teams List 2026

Updated October 3, 2026

The IPL teams list 2026 contains 10 franchises competing in the Indian Premier League: Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals, Gujarat Titans, Kolkata Knight Riders, Lucknow Super Giants, Mumbai Indians, Punjab Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Royal Challengers Bengaluru and Sunrisers Hyderabad. The official IPL site lists these 10 franchises, and the 2026 season began with the defending champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru.

IPL Teams and Their Details

1. Chennai Super Kings (CSK)

Chennai Super Kings represent Chennai and are one of the most successful IPL franchises. CSK have won five titles: 2010, 2011, 2018, 2021 and 2023. Their home venue is the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

2. Delhi Capitals (DC)

Delhi Capitals represent Delhi. The franchise has previously competed under the name Delhi Daredevils before adopting the Delhi Capitals name in 2019. The team has reached the IPL final but has not won the championship.

3. Gujarat Titans (GT)

Gujarat Titans represent Ahmedabad and entered the IPL in 2022. They won the championship in their debut season, defeating Rajasthan Royals in the 2022 final. Narendra Modi Stadium is the team's principal home venue.

4. Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR)

Kolkata Knight Riders represent Kolkata and have won three IPL titles: 2012, 2014 and 2024. Eden Gardens is their home venue.

5. Lucknow Super Giants (LSG)

Lucknow Super Giants represent Lucknow and joined the IPL in 2022. Their home venue is BRSABVE Cricket Stadium, commonly known as Ekana Cricket Stadium, in Lucknow. LSG have not won the IPL title.

6. Mumbai Indians (MI)

Mumbai Indians represent Mumbai and have won five IPL championships: 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019 and 2020. Their home ground is Wankhede Stadium.

7. Punjab Kings (PBKS)

Punjab Kings represent the Punjab franchise. The team previously played under the name Kings XI Punjab and adopted the Punjab Kings name in 2021. Their home matches have been staged at multiple venues, including the PCA Stadium in New Chandigarh and Dharamshala in recent seasons.

8. Rajasthan Royals (RR)

Rajasthan Royals represent Rajasthan, with Jaipur serving as their principal home city. RR won the inaugural IPL title in 2008. The franchise also uses the Barsapara Cricket Stadium in Guwahati for selected home matches.

9. Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB)

Royal Challengers Bengaluru represent Bengaluru. The franchise won its first IPL title in 2025 and successfully defended it in 2026, defeating Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the 2026 final. RCB became only the third franchise to retain the IPL trophy.

10. Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH)

Sunrisers Hyderabad represent Hyderabad and entered the IPL in 2013 as the successor franchise to Deccan Chargers. SRH won their only IPL title in 2016. Their home venue is Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium.

  • Delhi Daredevils → Delhi Capitals: The franchise adopted the Delhi Capitals name in 2019.
  • Kings XI Punjab → Punjab Kings: The franchise changed its name to Punjab Kings in 2021.
  • Royal Challengers Bangalore → Royal Challengers Bengaluru: The franchise officially adopted Bengaluru in its name in 2024.

How Many Teams Are There in IPL?

There are 10 teams in IPL 2026. The current league has remained a 10-team competition since Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants joined in 2022. The BCCI's 2026 season information lists all 10 franchises and describes the tournament as its 19th edition.

Which IPL Team Has Won the Most Titles?

Chennai Super Kings and Mumbai Indians have each won five IPL titles. Kolkata Knight Riders have three, while Royal Challengers Bengaluru have two after their 2025 and 2026 championships.

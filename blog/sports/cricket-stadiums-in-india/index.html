Cricket Stadiums in India

Updated October 2026

India has several major cricket stadiums that host Test matches, ODIs, T20Is, IPL games and major ICC tournaments. The Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad is the largest, with a BCCI-listed capacity of 1,32,000. Other prominent venues include Eden Gardens, Wankhede Stadium, M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, M.A. Chidambaram Stadium and Arun Jaitley Stadium.

Largest Cricket Stadiums in India

The Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad is the largest cricket stadium in India and is listed by BCCI at 1,32,000 seats. Eden Gardens in Kolkata is listed at 68,000, while the Ekana Stadium and Greenfield International Stadium are listed at 50,100 and 50,000 respectively.

1. Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad

The Narendra Modi Stadium is operated by the Gujarat Cricket Association and has a BCCI-listed capacity of 1,32,000. The venue was completely rebuilt between 2015 and 2020 and hosted the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2023 final. It also hosted the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 final on 8 March 2026.

2. Eden Gardens, Kolkata

Eden Gardens is one of India's most historic cricket grounds. BCCI lists its capacity as 68,000, and the venue has hosted major international matches and ICC events. It was the venue for the first semi-final of the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026.

3. Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai

Wankhede Stadium is operated by the Mumbai Cricket Association and is listed by BCCI at 33,100 seats. It hosted the 2011 Men's Cricket World Cup final, when India defeated Sri Lanka, and continues to host international cricket and IPL matches.

4. M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru

M. Chinnaswamy Stadium is located in central Bengaluru and is listed by BCCI at 33,800 capacity. It has been a regular venue for India's home international cricket and is also associated with the Karnataka State Cricket Association and IPL cricket.

5. M.A. Chidambaram Stadium, Chennai

Commonly called Chepauk, M.A. Chidambaram Stadium is one of India's established Test venues. BCCI lists its capacity at 38,200 and notes its long history, including India's first Test victory in 1952 and the tied Test between India and Australia in 1986.

6. Arun Jaitley Stadium, New Delhi

Located on Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg, the Arun Jaitley Stadium is one of India's historic international grounds. BCCI lists its capacity at 35,200 and records its association with major Test and ODI milestones.

7. Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium, Hyderabad

The Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium in Uppal is the major international cricket venue in Hyderabad. BCCI lists its capacity at 39,200 and notes that it replaced the Lal Bahadur Shastri Stadium as Hyderabad's principal international venue.

8. Other Major Cricket Stadiums in India

India's international cricket network also includes venues such as HPCA Stadium in Dharamshala, MCA International Stadium in Pune, Ekana Cricket Stadium in Lucknow, Greenfield International Stadium in Thiruvananthapuram, Barsapara Cricket Stadium in Guwahati, ACA-VDCA Cricket Stadium in Visakhapatnam and Barabati Stadium in Cuttack. These venues have hosted international matches and major domestic competitions.

Important Cricket Stadium GK Facts

  • Largest stadium: Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, with BCCI-listed capacity of 1,32,000.
  • Historic venue: Eden Gardens is one of India's oldest major cricket grounds and hosted the 1987 World Cup final.
  • 2011 World Cup final: Wankhede Stadium hosted the final between India and Sri Lanka.
  • 2026 T20 World Cup final: Narendra Modi Stadium hosted the final between India and New Zealand.
  • First Test win by India: M.A. Chidambaram Stadium hosted India's first Test victory in 1952.

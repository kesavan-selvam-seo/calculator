IPL Guide: Teams, Winners, Records, Rules and History

Updated October 17, 2026

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a franchise-based Twenty20 cricket competition organised by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI). The first season took place in 2008. This IPL guide brings together the league's teams, champions, records, rules, player awards and key season information in one place.

The latest completed edition, IPL 2026, ended on 31 May 2026. Royal Challengers Bengaluru defeated Gujarat Titans by five wickets in the final at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, and retained the title they won in 2025. Read the official IPL final report.

Most Wickets in International Cricket

Updated October 2026

Muttiah Muralitharan is the leading wicket-taker in men's international cricket, finishing his career with 1,347 wickets across Tests, ODIs and T20Is for Sri Lanka. He holds the men's records for most Test wickets (800) and most ODI wickets (534).

Who Has Taken the Most Wickets in International Cricket?

Muttiah Muralitharan leads men's international cricket with 1,347 wickets. ICC records 800 Test wickets, 534 ODI wickets and 13 T20I wickets for Sri Lanka.

Most Test Wickets

Muttiah Muralitharan is the leading Test wicket-taker with 800 wickets in 133 Tests. Shane Warne is second with 708, followed by James Anderson with 704. ICC's 2026 Test statistics also list Anil Kumble on 619 and Stuart Broad on 604.

Most Wickets in International Cricket: Important Records

  • Most men's international wickets: Muttiah Muralitharan – 1,347.
  • Most Test wickets: Muttiah Muralitharan – 800.
  • Most ODI wickets: Muttiah Muralitharan – 534.
  • Most wickets in men's World Cups: Muttiah Muralitharan took 68 in the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup.
  • Most Test wickets in a career by an Indian: Anil Kumble – 619.
  • Most Test wickets among England bowlers: James Anderson – 704.

Why Muralitharan Holds the International Wicket Record

Muralitharan combined an exceptionally long international career with elite wicket-taking in both Tests and ODIs. ICC records that he finished with 1,347 international wickets, 346 more than Shane Warne, who was second at the time of Muralitharan's retirement.

International Wickets vs IPL Wickets

International wickets are wickets taken for a national team in recognised international matches. IPL wickets are franchise-league statistics. They are separate categories and should not be added together when comparing international bowling records.

Men's and Women's International Wicket Records

Men's and women's international records are maintained as separate statistical categories. When publishing a women's wicket leaderboard, use the women's international record set rather than mixing it with the men's figures on this page.

IPL Teams and Their Home Grounds

Updated October 17, 2026

Each IPL franchise represents a city or region and normally plays home matches at a principal stadium. Teams may also use alternate venues in a season, so check the official fixture for a specific match.

Why IPL Venues Can Change

Scheduling, renovations, local arrangements or league decisions may move selected home games to an alternate ground. The principal home ground is a useful reference, but it does not guarantee that every home fixture will be played there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which stadium is CSK's home ground?

Chennai Super Kings' principal home ground is M. A. Chidambaram Stadium in Chennai.

Where do Gujarat Titans play home matches?

Their principal home venue is Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.

Do teams always play home matches at the listed stadium?

Not necessarily. Alternate venues may be announced for specific fixtures.

How to Calculate Percentages: Complete Percentage Calculation Guide

Percentage calculation is one of the most common calculations used in daily life, education, shopping, finance, business, and data comparison. A percentage shows how much one value represents out of 100. This makes it easier to compare numbers of different sizes.

Whether you are calculating exam marks, discount offers, profit margin, tax, interest, price increase, or percentage change, the basic idea remains the same. You compare a part with a total and convert the result into a value out of 100.

What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The word percentage means “per hundred.” For example, 40% means 40 out of 100. If a student scores 40 marks out of 100, the percentage is 40%.

Percentages are useful because they make comparison simple. Instead of comparing raw numbers, you can compare values on the same base of 100.

Percentage Formula

The basic percentage formula is simple and works for most percentage calculations.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

In this formula, the part is the value you want to compare, and the total is the full value. After dividing the part by the total, multiply the result by 100 to convert it into a percentage.

How to Calculate Percentage?

To calculate a percentage manually, divide the required value by the total value and multiply the answer by 100. This gives the percentage value.

  1. Identify the part value.
  2. Identify the total value.
  3. Divide the part by the total.
  4. Multiply the result by 100.
  5. Add the percentage symbol (%) after the answer.

Simple Percentage Calculation Example

Let us say you scored 72 marks out of 90. To calculate your percentage, divide 72 by 90 and multiply by 100.

Percentage = (72 ÷ 90) × 100 = 80%

How to Calculate Percentage of Marks?

To calculate percentage of marks, divide the marks obtained by the total marks and multiply the result by 100. This method is used in schools, colleges, entrance exams, and academic reports.

Marks Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

For example, if a student gets 425 marks out of 500, the percentage will be:

(425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

How to Calculate Percentage Increase?

Percentage increase is used when a value grows from an old value to a new value. It is commonly used for price increases, salary hikes, traffic growth, revenue growth, and performance comparison.

Percentage Increase = ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

For example, if a product price increases from ₹500 to ₹600, the increase is ₹100. Divide ₹100 by ₹500 and multiply by 100.

((600 - 500) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20%

So, the price increased by 20%.

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease?

Percentage decrease is used when a value becomes lower than the original value. It is useful for discount calculation, price drop, weight loss, traffic decline, and cost reduction.

Percentage Decrease = ((Old Value - New Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

For example, if a product price decreases from ₹1,000 to ₹800, the decrease is ₹200. Divide ₹200 by ₹1,000 and multiply by 100.

((1000 - 800) ÷ 1000) × 100 = 20%

So, the price decreased by 20%.

How to Calculate Discount Percentage?

Discount percentage tells how much price is reduced from the original price. It is useful while comparing offers during online shopping, retail sales, and festival discounts.

Discount Percentage = (Discount Amount ÷ Original Price) × 100

For example, if a product with an original price of ₹2,000 is sold for ₹1,500, the discount amount is ₹500.

(500 ÷ 2000) × 100 = 25%

So, the product has a 25% discount.

How to Calculate Percentage Difference?

Percentage difference is used to compare two values when there is no fixed old value or base value. It is commonly used in data comparison, research, business analysis, and measurement comparison.

Percentage Difference = (Difference ÷ Average of Two Values) × 100

For example, if two values are 80 and 100, the difference is 20 and the average is 90. The percentage difference is:

(20 ÷ 90) × 100 = 22.22%

Percentage Change Formula

Percentage change shows how much a value has changed compared to its original value. It can be positive or negative based on whether the value increased or decreased.

Percentage Change = ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100

If the answer is positive, it is a percentage increase. If the answer is negative, it is a percentage decrease.

Percentage in Finance

Percentages are very important in finance. Interest rates, loan rates, investment returns, profit margins, and tax rates are usually shown in percentage form. For example, if a bank gives 8% annual interest, it means the interest is calculated as 8 out of every 100 for one year.

Common Percentage Mistakes to Avoid

Many users make mistakes while calculating percentages manually. The most common mistake is using the wrong base value. In percentage increase and decrease, the old value should usually be used as the base.

  • Do not confuse percentage points with percentage change.
  • Always use the correct total value as the base.
  • For increase and decrease, compare with the old value.
  • Check whether the question asks for percentage, ratio, or difference.
  • Use decimal values carefully while calculating financial percentages.

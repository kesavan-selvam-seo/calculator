How Many Candidates Have Registered for JEE Main 2027 So Far?

As per the latest registration update reported on 20 November 2026, JEE Main 2027 Session 1 had received 12,23,255 submitted application forms. Of these, 10,10,941 candidates had completed the application fee payment. The application process was still underway at the time of this update, so these figures should not be treated as the final registration count for the 2027 examination.

12,23,255Session 1 application forms submitted
10,10,941Session 1 fee payments completed
Important: The number of forms submitted is not the same as the number of confirmed registrations. An application with pending fee payment may not be considered complete. NTA's final result statistics will provide the confirmed session-wise and unique-candidate totals.

The latest figures are reported in the 20 November 2026 registration update by Shiksha's JEE Main 2027 registration report. CalcuPortal will update this page when NTA publishes a newer count or the final statistics.

JEE Main 2027 Forms Submitted vs Fee Payment Completed

When checking registration numbers, it is important to distinguish between an application form being submitted and the fee payment being completed. These two figures may differ while the registration window is open.

Applicants should log in to the official portal and verify their own payment and confirmation status.

Previous-Year JEE Main Registration Statistics

The previous-year figures help put the current application activity into context. NTA's final 2026 statistics show that 16,04,854 unique candidates registered across the January and April sessions. A candidate who registered for both sessions is counted once in the unique-candidate total.

JEE Main 2027 Session 1 and Session 2 Registration

JEE Main is conducted in two sessions in the January and April examination cycle. Candidates can apply for one session or both, subject to the application schedule and instructions issued by NTA. The better of the two session scores is considered for the final result, as stated on the official examination information page.

  • Session 1: Registration is underway in November 2026. The figures on this page refer to the Session 1 application snapshot reported on 20 November 2026.
  • Session 2: The separate application window and final registration statistics should be checked on the official JEE Main website when NTA publishes the schedule.
  • Unique candidates: The final combined total counts a candidate once even if they register for both sessions.

For the latest dates, application notices and candidate instructions, visit the official JEE Main website and the NTA JEE examination page.

How to Check the Latest JEE Main 2027 Registration Updates

  1. Visit the official JEE Main website.
  2. Open the latest public notice or candidate activity related to JEE Main 2027.
  3. Check the application schedule, fee-payment deadline and any revised instructions.
  4. For your own application, log in and verify the confirmation page and payment status.
  5. Use NTA's result press releases for final registered, appeared and unique-candidate statistics.
Do not rely on unofficial estimates as final totals. Registration numbers can change until the application window closes and NTA completes its verification and reporting.

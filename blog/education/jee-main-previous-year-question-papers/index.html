JEE Main Previous Year Question Papers PDF: Official NTA Sources

JEE Main previous year questions (PYQs) are authentic questions administered in earlier NTA examination sessions. They are useful for identifying question formats and common concept combinations. This page prioritises officially hosted NTA files rather than treating a coaching PDF as an original government source.

JEE Main 2026 Answer Key and Response Sheet

An actual exam question paper and an official final answer key serve different purposes. The paper contains the questions. A final answer key identifies the accepted answer identifiers or responses under NTA's evaluation, and sometimes a public response-sheet notice includes additional candidate-specific material.

  1. Open NTA's Documents archive and locate the 2026 Session 1 / Session 2 final answer keys.
  2. Match the correct paper, date, session and question/option IDs.
  3. Review the answer-key instructions and amendments rather than assuming a third-party solution is authoritative.
  4. For older user-specific recorded responses, only use a legitimate NTA Candidate Activity account when that service is open; do not enter credentials on CalcuPortal.

The official 2026 public notices list final answer keys for both sessions. Answer keys for JEE Main 2027 will be published only after the applicable 2027 examination and review process.

How to Solve JEE Main PYQs for 2027 Preparation

  1. Check the latest 2027 syllabus: open the 2027 subject-wise syllabus guide and NTA's original PDF.
  2. Start chapter-wise: use past questions on topics you have recently revised, checking each solution carefully.
  3. Progress to a complete shift paper: sit a timed three-hour session, using current 2027 pattern rules where relevant.
  4. Score with care: use the official marking rules for that specific past paper, noting changes across years.
  5. Track mistakes: separate concept gaps, time pressure, reading errors and calculation mistakes.
  6. Retest weak concepts: spend time on unseen problems, not memorising the answer to the same PYQ.

Why shift matters: questions and difficulty vary across sessions and days. A raw score on a 2026 shift is a useful practice datapoint but not a promise of 2027 marks, percentile, cutoff or rank.

Physics, Chemistry and Maths JEE Main PYQ Practice

Maths PYQs

Focus on algebraic reasoning, calculus procedures, geometry and time allocation. Reproduce solutions clearly before speed work.

Physics PYQs

Check unit conversions, diagrams, formula selection and physical assumptions. Practise numerical and conceptual items.

Chemistry PYQs

Alternate physical calculation, organic concepts and inorganic recall as permitted by the latest syllabus.

When extracting one subject from a complete shift paper, keep the original question number and any chart or illustration intact. Avoid assuming that an old question proves the associated topic is still in the 2027 syllabus; always cross-check NTA's 2027 PDF.

How Many JEE Main Previous Papers Should I Solve?

No single number of papers guarantees a target score. A useful plan is to start with one recent 2026 paper to identify weak areas, then practise a mix of recent shift papers and chapter-wise PYQs over multiple weeks. Record your score, incorrect topics and time spent each time.

JEE Main PYQs vs JEE Advanced Question Papers

JEE Main Paper 1 and JEE Advanced are different examinations with distinct question styles and marking rules. JEE Main PYQs help with NTA Paper 1 preparation. For IIT-focused advanced preparation, consult official JEE Advanced past papers and our JEE Advanced 2027 guide.

Also use the JEE Main 2027 original practice quiz if you want a quick diagnostic before a full three-hour paper.

JEE Main Previous Year Question Paper FAQs

Where can I download official JEE Main PYQ PDFs?

Use NTA's official JEE Main 2026 archive. This guide also links to four individually verified official B.Tech April 2026 question-paper PDFs.

Are JEE Main 2027 previous-year question papers available?

No 2027 JEE Main exam paper existed as of 27 October 2026 because the 2027 examinations have not taken place. Prepare with 2026 and earlier real papers.

Do the PDFs on this page contain official NTA question papers?

Yes. The four listed 2026 shift papers are linked directly to NTA-hosted government document URLs. They are genuine previous-year papers, not CalcuPortal-made practice sets.

Where can I get JEE Main 2026 answer keys?

NTA's Documents and Current Events sections include the final answer-key notices for Session 1 and Session 2 in 2026. Always match the correct paper and shift.

How should I analyse JEE Main previous-year questions?

Solve a complete paper under timed conditions, check relevant answers, log the reason for each error, and revisit weak syllabus units before another mock.

Can I calculate my exact percentile from a PYQ practice score?

No. A raw score on a previous-year paper is not an official NTA percentile; percentile depends on the actual session's candidate performance distribution.

Which subject should I practise first: Physics, Chemistry or Maths?

Choose based on your weak topics and timing. Over several weeks, include all three subjects rather than practising only one.

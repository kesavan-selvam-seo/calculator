CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027: Latest Official Update

The CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027 has been released. The Central Board of Secondary Education published the combined Class X and Class XII Board Examination timetable on 6 November 2026. The Class 12 theory examinations will be held from 16 February to 9 April 2027. All papers commence at 10:30 AM (IST), while the paper duration varies by subject.

How PCM students can prepare

  • Physics: revise concepts, derivations, numerical applications and practical records.
  • Chemistry: practise physical, organic and inorganic chemistry questions using the current syllabus.
  • Mathematics: solve complete papers under timed conditions and review each step against the marking scheme.

How PCB students can prepare

Biology students can revise diagrams, key processes and structured answers. Students also preparing for NEET should keep entrance-exam practice distinct from CBSE answer-writing and practical requirements. Use the official Class XII sample papers and marking schemes for the registered subject.

CBSE Class 12 Practical Exam Date 2027 and Project Assessments

CBSE has issued separate instructions for practical examinations, project assessments and internal assessments for the 2026–27 session. These dates are separate from the Class XII theory timetable.

School categoryPractical / project / internal assessment dates

The winter-bound school dates are specified in CBSE's circular dated 5 November 2026. The regular-session schedule is stated in the CBSE subject-wise marks bifurcation circular dated 5 November 2026. Students should follow the timetable issued by their own school and any later board instructions.

Practical examination preparation checklist

  1. Confirm the subject-wise practical or project date with your school.
  2. Complete laboratory records, files and project submissions according to the 2026–27 syllabus.
  3. Review the assessment pattern and viva requirements for each subject.
  4. Check that your registered subject and candidate details are correct.
  5. Keep practical and theory revision plans separate.

How to use the official marking schemes

  • Identify section-wise question types and marks using the paper for your specific subject.
  • Attempt the full SQP with a suitable timed practice session.
  • Review the official marking scheme and note exactly what a complete response requires.
  • Track calculation, interpretation, diagram, presentation or writing errors.
  • Repeat high-priority weak areas from the current syllabus rather than studying outdated chapters.

Balancing board exams with entrance preparation

Science students may also prepare for JEE or NEET, while students across streams may consider CUET or other entrance tests. Use a realistic weekly plan, but preserve separate time for CBSE answer-writing formats, practical requirements and school assignments. Refer to the confirmed entrance-exam schedules separately.

  • Revise every registered subject regularly, not only the next paper.
  • Use timed practice and review mistakes after each mock test.
  • Alternate theory-heavy and problem-solving subjects.
  • Follow school instructions for practicals and internal assessments.
  • Protect sleep and include short breaks during revision.

How to Verify the Official CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027

Use the board-hosted timetable to confirm your paper dates. Third-party summaries may omit electives or show only commonly searched subjects.

  1. Year and exam: confirm that the PDF is for the 2027 Class XII main board examination.
  2. Issuer: open the PDF from the official CBSE website or examination circulars.
  3. Subject code: match every paper with your school registration and admit card.
  4. Paper timing: check whether your subject ends at 12:30 PM or 1:30 PM.
  5. Revisions: revisit CBSE notices for any subsequent corrections or rescheduling.
  6. Practical schedule: follow the separate practical / project circular and your school's confirmed timetable.

Class 10 policy is not the Class 12 timetable. The two-board-examination arrangement for Class X should not be described as two compulsory Class XII main examinations. Class XII supplementary or special examinations are governed by separate CBSE notices.

CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027: Frequently Asked Questions

Is the CBSE Class 12 date sheet 2027 released?

Yes. CBSE released the Class X and Class XII Board Examination 2027 date sheet on 6 November 2026. The official PDF is available on the CBSE website.

When will the CBSE Class 12 board exams 2027 start and end?

The Class XII theory examinations begin on 16 February 2027 and conclude on 9 April 2027.

What is the CBSE Class 12 Physics exam date 2027?

Physics (subject code 042) is scheduled for 16 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is the CBSE Class 12 Chemistry exam date 2027?

Chemistry (subject code 043) is scheduled for 27 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is the CBSE Class 12 Mathematics exam date 2027?

Mathematics (041) and Applied Mathematics (241) are scheduled for 9 March 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is the CBSE Class 12 English exam date 2027?

English Elective (001) and English Core (301) are scheduled for 5 March 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is the CBSE Class 12 Accountancy exam date 2027?

Accountancy (subject code 055) is scheduled for 23 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What is the CBSE Class 12 Business Studies exam date 2027?

Business Studies (subject code 054) is scheduled for 1 April 2027, from 10:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

What time do CBSE Class 12 board exams start in 2027?

All papers in the official date sheet commence at 10:30 AM IST. The duration varies by subject.

When are CBSE Class 12 practical exams for 2027?

CBSE's circulars schedule practicals for winter-bound schools from 9 November to 19 December 2026 and for regular-session schools from 1 January to 13 January 2027. Follow your school's subject-wise timetable.

Does CBSE conduct two compulsory Class 12 board exams in 2027?

No. The two-board-examination policy for Class X does not automatically apply to Class XII. The Class XII main examination and any supplementary or special examination are separate arrangements.

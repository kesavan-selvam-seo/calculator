When Will CBSE Release the Class 10 Date Sheet 2027?

How to Download the CBSE Class 10 Date Sheet 2027 PDF

  1. Visit the official CBSE website.
  2. Look for the latest examination updates or date sheet notification.
  3. Open the notice for the Class 10 / Secondary Board Examination 2027.
  4. Open the PDF and check each subject name, exam date and reporting or paper timing instructions.
  5. Download or print the official PDF for reference.
  6. Recheck the CBSE website for any revised date sheet or subsequent notice.

The download link will be available from CBSE after the board publishes the schedule. Until then, avoid using unofficial PDFs as a final timetable.

Which Subjects Should Students Check in the Date Sheet?

When the timetable is released, check the date against the exact subjects and course options registered through your school. Depending on the student's subject combination, the schedule may include:

  • Mathematics Basic or Mathematics Standard
  • Science
  • Social Science
  • English Language and Literature or English Communicative
  • Hindi Course A or Hindi Course B
  • Information Technology, Computer Applications, Artificial Intelligence and other skill or elective subjects, where applicable

This is a general list of common subject options, not the official 2027 timetable. Refer to your school's registered subject combination and the CBSE PDF for the exact schedule.

Will CBSE Conduct Two Class 10 Board Exams in 2027?

CBSE introduced a two-board-examination system for Class X beginning with the 2026 examinations. The board's official policy notification dated 25 June 2025 explains the framework, and a February 2026 clarification addresses eligibility.

Is the first exam compulsory?

Under the published policy, appearing in the first/main examination is mandatory for students generally. The scheme contains defined special-category arrangements, including provisions for certain sports students and winter-bound schools. Students should review the relevant circular and their school's instructions rather than assuming the second examination can simply replace the first.

Who may take the second exam?

For candidates who satisfy the published requirements, the second examination can provide an improvement opportunity in up to three specified main subjects or a route for eligible compartment candidates. The first and second examinations cover the full annual syllabus. Internal assessment is conducted once under the scheme.

The 2026 clarification also explains that students absent in three or more subjects in the first examination are generally not eligible to use the second examination as a substitute and may be placed in an Essential Repeat category. Consult any updated 2027 notification for the rules applicable to your specific circumstances.

Two examinations do not mean two compulsory attempts for every student. The first exam is the main examination. The second is a defined opportunity subject to CBSE eligibility, category, registration and official scheduling rules.

Subject-wise preparation priorities

  • Mathematics: work through concepts and exercises, then practise full solutions with the steps required by marking schemes.
  • Science: revise key principles, diagrams, applications and calculations across the prescribed course.
  • Social Science: connect concepts and events, revise maps where relevant and practise structured written answers.
  • English and languages: balance reading comprehension, writing formats, grammar and literature revision.
  • Skill and optional subjects: consult the exact official syllabus, sample paper and school instructions instead of relying on another subject's assessment pattern.

Once the official date sheet arrives, put the actual examination order into the study calendar. Prioritise earlier exams while keeping revision slots for later ones. Take regular breaks and leave room for school instructions and changes to the schedule.

What Students and Parents Should Check After the Date Sheet Is Released

The first timetable PDF is an important milestone, but students should also verify registration information and later examination instructions. A small discrepancy in subject code or assessment category can be more consequential than a rumour about an exam date.

  1. Confirm the official PDF clearly refers to Class X and 2027, not the prior year's date sheet.
  2. Verify the examination phase: first/main or second, where applicable.
  3. Match all subject names and registered subject codes with school records and later admit-card details.
  4. Record paper dates, reporting-time instructions and permitted materials from official communications.
  5. Check school messages for internal assessment, practical/project requirements and administrative deadlines.
  6. Revisit CBSE's website before exam days in case a revised date sheet or important advisory is issued.

Important distinction: CBSE's Class 10 board examinations are different from state-board schedules, including the Tamil Nadu SSLC timetable. Always check that an article or PDF refers to CBSE, the correct class and the correct academic year.

JEE Main 2027 Mathematics Syllabus Study Areas

Mathematics benefits from combining algebraic fluency, coordinate geometry and calculus. Practise numerical methods and concept applications rather than memorising only formulas.

  • Sets, relations and functions
  • Complex numbers and quadratic equations
  • Matrices and determinants
  • Permutations and combinations
  • Binomial theorem and sequences
  • Limits, continuity and differentiability
  • Applications of derivatives
  • Integral calculus and differential equations
  • Coordinate geometry: straight lines and circles
  • Conic sections
  • Vectors and three-dimensional geometry
  • Statistics and probability
  • Trigonometry

For the exact 2027 Mathematics units, refer to NTA's current official PDF; the list above is a broad revision taxonomy.

JEE Main 2027 Physics Syllabus Study Areas

Physics preparation should connect definitions and laws to calculations. Work through units and dimensions, free-body diagrams, field equations and qualitative reasoning.

  • Units, dimensions and error analysis
  • Kinematics and laws of motion
  • Work, energy and power
  • Rotational motion and gravitation
  • Properties of solids, liquids and gases
  • Thermodynamics and kinetic theory
  • Oscillations and waves
  • Electrostatics and capacitance
  • Current electricity
  • Magnetism and electromagnetic induction
  • Alternating current and electromagnetic waves
  • Optics: ray and wave
  • Dual nature, atoms and nuclei
  • Electronic devices and experiments

Use a labelling system to record errors as conceptual, formula-selection, calculation or unit mistakes.

JEE Main 2027 Chemistry Syllabus Study Areas

Separate your Chemistry revision into physical, inorganic and organic strands, then practise mixed questions. Verify chapter inclusions in the official NTA 2027 document.

  • Basic mole calculations and stoichiometry
  • Atomic structure
  • Chemical bonding and molecular structure
  • Chemical thermodynamics
  • Equilibrium and ionic equilibria
  • Redox reactions and electrochemistry
  • Solutions and chemical kinetics
  • Classification and periodic trends
  • p-block, d-block and f-block concepts
  • Coordination compounds
  • Organic basics, isomerism and mechanisms
  • Hydrocarbons and oxygen-containing organic compounds
  • Nitrogen-containing organic compounds
  • Biomolecules and practical chemistry

Pay attention to numerical practice, chemical notation, reaction patterns and the latest prescribed syllabus boundaries.

How to Complete the JEE Main 2027 Syllabus

  1. Download the definitive PDF. Use only the units present in the current NTA syllabus as the scope of your plan.
  2. Tag every chapter: not started, partly prepared or solved confidently under timed conditions.
  3. Close conceptual gaps: revise textbooks and worked examples before practising faster variants.
  4. Mix old and new units: include earlier chapters in weekly reviews to prevent forgetting.

JEE Main Syllabus FAQs

Is JEE Main 2027 official syllabus released?

Yes. NTA publishes an official 2027 syllabus page on jeemain.nta.nic.in. Use that document for the definitive list of units.

Is JEE Main 2027 syllabus the same as 2026?

Published comparisons of the 2027 and 2026 NTA syllabuses report no additions or removals. Paper 1 has 14 Maths, 20 Physics and 20 Chemistry units. Confirm the exact topics in NTA's official 2027 PDF.

What subjects are in JEE Main Paper 1?

Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics. Under the current pattern each subject contributes 25 questions and a maximum of 100 marks.

Does JEE Main 2027 syllabus include Class 11 and Class 12 chapters?

Preparation spans senior-secondary mathematics, physics and chemistry concepts. Consult the exact NTA syllabus for the prescribed units rather than treating school class boundaries as its only definition.

Where can I download JEE Main 2027 syllabus PDF?

Visit https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/document/syllabus-2027/ and open the NTA-hosted View/Download document.

Is B.Arch / B.Planning syllabus the same as B.Tech?

No. Paper 2A includes aptitude and drawing; Paper 2B includes aptitude and planning topics. Check the relevant sections of NTA's official syllabus.

Can I use your progress checklist as an official syllabus?

No. It is a broad, editable-in-browser revision checklist, not a verbatim reproduction of every 2027 NTA syllabus unit.

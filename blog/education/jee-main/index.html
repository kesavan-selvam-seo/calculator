Class 12 Science Date Sheet 2027: PCM and PCB

For students studying PCM (Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics) or PCB (Physics, Chemistry and Biology), practical work and subject-specific time gaps make a correct board timetable particularly important. The Class 12 Science 2027 exam dates for Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology and English have not been announced.

How PCM students can prepare now

  • Physics: revise conceptual applications, calculations, derivations and your prescribed practical records.
  • Chemistry: practise inorganic, organic and physical chemistry topics in the current 2026–27 syllabus; use its paper pattern.
  • Mathematics: solve full solutions under timed conditions and check the official marking scheme; use Applied Mathematics materials only if registered for that course.
  • English: continue reading and writing practice even while concentrating on the three core science subjects.

How PCB students can prepare now

Biology students can revise labelled diagrams, key processes and short- and long-form responses with the official marking scheme. Keep a separate timetable for NEET-oriented preparation where relevant, but do not claim a definite gap between board papers or an entrance examination until the authorised schedules are released.

The official Class XII sample-paper and marking-scheme page provides links for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and other subjects.

Class 12 Commerce Date Sheet 2027: Accountancy, Economics and Business Studies

The CBSE Class 12 Commerce date sheet 2027 has not been published for Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, English or related electives. Commerce combinations differ between schools, especially when students select Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Entrepreneurship or Informatics Practices.

  • Accountancy: practise full-format questions, calculations and presentation of working notes.
  • Business Studies: connect each concept with its applications and practise clear, structured answers.
  • Economics: revise core concepts, diagrams and numerical questions according to the prescribed curriculum.
  • Mathematics / Applied Mathematics: use only the official syllabus and sample papers matching the registered subject.
  • English: schedule continuous comprehension and writing practice rather than postponing language study.

Use the CBSE 2026–27 SQP page to access Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Applied Mathematics and English marking schemes. Once CBSE releases the time table, organise revision around your actual paper order—not an imagined “Commerce exam week”.

CBSE Class 12 Practical Exam Date 2027 and Project Assessments

Many Class XII courses include practical examinations, project work or internal assessment. The practical timetable is not identical to the main theory-paper timetable, and individual schools may communicate dates based on CBSE's prescribed assessment window and external examiner arrangements.

As historical context, CBSE's prior cycles placed practical and internal assessment activity earlier for winter-bound schools and before theory examinations for other schools. Broad periods such as November–December for winter-bound schools and January–February for others have appeared in previous cycles, but this article does not treat those as confirmed 2026–27 school-specific dates.

Practical examination preparation checklist

  1. Ask the school for its official subject-wise practical / project dates and reporting instructions.
  2. Complete laboratory records, files and project submissions using the applicable 2026–27 syllabus.
  3. Check the subject's marking pattern and viva preparation requirements.
  4. Verify your candidate and subject information with the school examination team.
  5. Keep practical and theory revision plans separately, allowing for any officially scheduled changes.

Do not confuse a practical examination window with a confirmed Physics, Chemistry or Biology theory exam date.

Balancing board exams with entrance preparation

Science students may also prepare for JEE or NEET, while students across streams may consider CUET or other entrance tests. Use an integrated weekly study plan where topics overlap, but preserve distinct slots for CBSE's answer-writing formats, practicals and school assignments. Do not rearrange a month's plan around an unverified online “2027 date sheet”.

Easy weekly approach

  • Allocate regular revision time to every registered subject, not just the hardest one.
  • Use one recurring slot for correcting errors from mock papers.
  • Alternate content-heavy and problem-solving subjects to reduce fatigue.
  • Complete internal assessment records according to the school calendar.
  • Take adequate breaks and protect consistent sleep during exam preparation.

How to Verify a Genuine Class 12 Date Sheet PDF

Older or unrelated CBSE PDFs are often circulated with a fresh 2027 headline. Before sharing one with classmates, check the document carefully.

  1. Year: the document should explicitly state the 2027 Class XII main examination, not the 2026 cycle.
  2. Issuer: verify the document is accessible from CBSE's own website or examination circulars.
  3. Exam type: distinguish main examinations from supplementary, private-candidate instructions or the 2026 West Asia special examinations.
  4. Subject code: compare each paper with your registration and admit card.
  5. Latest revision: check subsequent circulars for rescheduled papers and updated instructions.
  6. Paper timing: read the exact timing and duration specified for your subject.

Class 10 policy ≠ Class 12 policy. The two-board-examination arrangement introduced for Class X must not be described as two compulsory or automatically available Class XII main examinations. Class XII supplementary or special exams are governed by separate circulars.

CBSE Class 12 Date Sheet 2027: Frequently Asked Questions

Is the CBSE Class 12 date sheet 2027 released?

As checked on 25 October 2026, CBSE had not published the subject-wise timetable for the main Class XII Board Examinations 2027. Check the official CBSE announcements and examination circulars for updates.

When will the CBSE Class 12 board exams 2027 start?

No exact start date has been announced for the main 2027 exams. The 2026 main examinations began on 17 February, so a February 2027 start is a planning expectation, not an officially confirmed date.

Where can I download the CBSE 12th date sheet 2027 PDF?

Once CBSE publishes it, use cbse.gov.in and open the Class XII 2027 main examination date-sheet notice or its latest revised version. A verified direct PDF for the main Class 12 exam 2027 was not available on 25 October 2026.

Are Class 12 Science, Commerce and Arts exam dates the same?

CBSE releases subject-wise examination dates rather than one identical timetable for every stream. The exact dates for Physics, Chemistry, Accountancy, History and other papers depend on the official Class XII date sheet and registered subjects.

When will Class 12 practical exams take place in 2027?

The precise practical and project-assessment timetable must be confirmed by CBSE and each school. Historically, winter-bound school practicals have been scheduled toward November-December and those in other schools before the main theory examinations, often January-February.

What is the official Class 12 2026–27 sample paper link?

CBSE Academic lists Class XII 2026–27 Sample Question Papers and Marking Schemes at CBSE Academic's official Class XII SQP page.

Does CBSE conduct two compulsory Class 12 board exams in 2027?

The two-board-examination policy introduced for the 2026 examination cycle applies to Class X, not automatically to Class XII. The Class XII main examination and any supplementary or special examination are distinct arrangements.

Is the 2026 Class 12 date sheet valid for 2027?

No. The 2026 timetable is historical reference only. CBSE published a tentative 2026 date sheet on 24 September 2025, a date sheet on 30 October 2025 and later revisions. It cannot be reused as the 2027 subject schedule.

How can I find the exam time for my Class 12 subject?

When the Class XII 2027 date sheet is available, verify the exact subject, its code, paper date, reporting time and duration in the CBSE PDF and your admit card. Do not assume that every paper has the same finishing time.

Are Class 12 private candidate and West Asia special exam notices the regular 2027 date sheet?

No. The October 2026 Class XII West Asia special examination notice relates to a 2026 special examination, and 2026–27 private-candidate forms are registration notices. Neither is the main Class XII 2027 date sheet.

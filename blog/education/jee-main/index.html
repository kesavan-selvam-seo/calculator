How to Apply for JEE Main 2027: NTA JEE Registration Form

The JEE Main 2027 registration form is online at jeemain.nta.nic.in. The process includes account registration, detailed application, document upload, fee payment and confirmation. Do not use unofficial agents asking for your password or OTP.

  1. Open the NTA portal. Choose “Online Application Form for JEE (Main) 2027 – Session 1” in Candidate Activity.
  2. Read the official information bulletin. Verify eligibility, the chosen paper, acceptable identification and upload specifications.
  3. Create your login. Submit the required personal details, mobile and email verification and save your official application number.
  4. Complete the application form. Carefully provide qualifying examination, category, address and other requested information.
  5. Select the paper and exam preferences. Paper 1 serves B.E./B.Tech; Paper 2A B.Arch; Paper 2B B.Planning.
  6. Upload required images and certificates. Follow the 2027 on-screen file size and format requirements.
  7. Pay the official fee. Check payment success inside the NTA portal; a bank debit alone is not your confirmation page.
  8. Download the confirmation page. Save a PDF with your application number and payment reference for later notices.

Is 75% Class 12 marks compulsory?

Eligibility to sit JEE Main is not identical to eligibility for admission. Board marks or top-20-percentile rules can apply to particular NITs, IIITs and CFTIs under admission rules; IIT eligibility through JEE Advanced is separate. Do not assume the 75% rule uniformly prevents every student from filling the JEE form. Check JoSAA counselling rules and the current NTA bulletin.

JEE Main 2027 Exam Pattern: 75 Questions, 300 Marks

Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) is a computer-based examination of 75 questions for a maximum of 300 marks, normally lasting three hours. There are 20 multiple-choice questions and five numerical-value questions in each subject.

Under the reported 2027 marking pattern, a correct response earns +4, a wrong MCQ or numerical response receives −1 and an unanswered item receives 0. Read NTA's original question-paper instructions for the relevant session.

Score example: 48 correct answers, 12 wrong answers and 15 unattempted gives 48×4 − 12 = 180/300. That is 60% of maximum raw marks, not 60 NTA percentile.

JEE Main 2027 City Slip, Admit Card and Exam Day

The advance city intimation slip and the JEE Main admit card are separate documents. The admit card, when issued, gives your assigned date, shift, examination centre, required ID and other instructions.

  1. Open NTA's JEE Main portal after the corresponding session announcement.
  2. Use only official candidate activity login to download the document.
  3. Check name, photo, paper, centre address, exam date, time and reporting instructions.
  4. Take the original documents required by NTA and respect its security and allowed-items rules.
  5. If anything is wrong, use the official NTA helpdesk details from the current bulletin.

As of 25 October 2026, the 2027 January admit card is not live. Published planning dates indicate January 13 for initial availability, subject to the actual NTA release; avoid fake “download now” claims.

JEE Main 2027 Answer Key, Result, Percentile and Cutoff

NTA publishes answer keys, challenge procedures, NTA Scores and results through separate notices after each session. No official 2027 result or answer-key link is live at the time of this article.

Raw percentage vs JEE Main percentile

180/300 = 60% raw marks, but not a 60 percentile. NTA Scores depend on how many candidates in the applicable session/shift scored at or below a given score, plus the authority's normalisation procedure. An exact NTA percentile cannot be calculated from a candidate's marks without the relevant score distribution.

JEE Advanced qualifying cutoff vs JoSAA closing rank

The cutoff required to qualify for JEE Advanced, a college's admission eligibility and the final branch-wise closing rank from JoSAA are different measures. Seat availability, candidate category, quota, choice filling and institute rules all matter. Use JoSAA for counselling and JEE Advanced for IIT criteria.

JEE Main 2027 Frequently Asked Questions

Is JEE Main 2027 registration open?

Yes, NTA opened JEE Main 2027 Session 1 online registration on 24 October 2026.

What is the last date for JEE Main 2027 registration?

The regular Session 1 registration deadline is 22 November 2026, with a notified late registration period from 23 to 26 November 2026.

When is JEE Main 2027 Session 1?

When is JEE Main 2027 Session 2?

Who can apply for JEE Main 2027?

The qualifying-examination eligibility includes candidates who passed Class 12 or equivalent in 2025 or 2026 or are appearing in 2027, subject to the full 2027 bulletin.

What is the JEE Main 2027 exam pattern?

B.E./B.Tech Paper 1 is a 3-hour, 300-mark computer-based examination with 75 questions: 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical questions each in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

Is the JEE Main 2027 syllabus released?

Yes. NTA has published a JEE Main 2027 syllabus page on jeemain.nta.nic.in.

What is the NTA JEE Main marking scheme?

Paper 1 gives 4 marks for a correct answer and deducts 1 mark for an incorrect MCQ or numerical answer, according to the published 2027 pattern.

Is 75% in Class 12 required to fill JEE Main form?

Eligibility to appear for JEE Main differs from eligibility for admission to NITs, IIITs, CFTIs and IITs. Verify institution-specific admission rules and the official bulletin.

Can I calculate exact percentile from JEE Main marks?

No. Raw marks percentage is not NTA percentile; exact NTA Scores depend on the distribution of candidates' marks in the relevant session and shift.

How do I apply for JEE Main 2027?

Visit the official NTA portal, register, complete candidate and academic details, upload required documents, pay the examination fee and save the confirmation page.

Where can I download the JEE Main 2027 admit card?

When released, the admit card will be available through NTA's official candidate activity links at jeemain.nta.nic.in.

What is the difference between JEE Main and JEE Advanced?

JEE Main is administered by NTA and is used for admissions at NITs, IIITs and participating institutions and for JEE Advanced shortlisting. JEE Advanced is a separate IIT entrance examination.

Bangalore to Ooty Distance, Route & Travel Time

Find the Bangalore to Ooty distance, best road routes, realistic travel time, transport options, fuel cost and practical tips for planning the journey.

The Bangalore to Ooty distance is roughly 270–290 km by road, depending on where you start in Bengaluru and which route you take. A direct drive usually takes around 6–8 hours in normal conditions, but traffic, breaks, forest-road restrictions and the final climb into the Nilgiris can make the journey longer.

Bangalore to Ooty Distance at a Glance

For most travellers, the useful figure to remember is about 270–290 km by road. The exact distance changes with your starting point in Bengaluru, the destination address in Ooty and the route selected by your navigation app. Some route planners may show a slightly different figure, so treat the distance as a planning range rather than a fixed number.

Best Route from Bangalore to Ooty

The most commonly used road corridor runs through Mysuru, Gundlupet, Bandipur and Gudalur before reaching Ooty. It combines the Bengaluru–Mysuru highway with forest roads and the hill section leading into the Nilgiris.

Route: Bengaluru → Ramanagara → Mysuru → Nanjangud/Gundlupet → Bandipur → Gudalur → Ooty.

The first part of the journey is comparatively straightforward. After Mysuru and Gundlupet, the landscape changes as you enter the forest belt around Bandipur. From the forest region towards Gudalur and the Nilgiris, the road becomes more winding and the final climb needs slower, more attentive driving.

Karnataka Tourism places Bandipur on the Mysuru–Ooty road and describes it as part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve. That makes this stretch more than just a connection between two cities: it is also a sensitive forest corridor where drivers should follow posted restrictions and avoid unnecessary stopping.

Why this route is popular

  • It connects Bengaluru and the Nilgiris through the major Mysuru corridor.
  • It offers a combination of highway driving, forest scenery and mountain roads.
  • Mysuru provides an easy place for a breakfast or short break.
  • Gundlupet is useful for fuel and supplies before the longer forest stretch.
  • The route works well for both self-drive trips and hired cabs.

Bangalore to Ooty Travel Time

For a road trip, 6–8 hours is a practical planning range under reasonably good conditions. A navigation app may show a shorter driving estimate when traffic is light, but that does not mean the same time will hold for a weekend, holiday or long break.

For a family trip or a single-driver journey, it is sensible to keep a buffer rather than planning your arrival to the minute. Weather, traffic, road works and stops can all change the actual travel time.

Best Stops on the Way from Bangalore to Ooty

Mysuru

Mysuru is the natural first major break on the route. If you leave Bengaluru early, it is a convenient place for breakfast, coffee or a short rest before continuing towards Gundlupet and Bandipur.

Gundlupet

Gundlupet is useful as a practical stop for fuel, food and last-minute supplies before continuing towards the forest corridor. If your vehicle needs fuel, avoid leaving it until the next major town.

Bandipur

Bandipur is one of the most distinctive sections of the journey. The forest is part of a larger protected landscape associated with the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve. Keep your speed under control, do not disturb wildlife and follow the instructions displayed along the route.

Gudalur and the Nilgiris

After the forest section, the landscape gradually changes to the Nilgiri hill environment. This is where the journey becomes more winding, so keep the final part of the drive relaxed rather than trying to make up lost time.

Bangalore to Ooty by Bus, Train and Flight

Driving is not the only way to travel. Your best option depends on whether you value flexibility, convenience, cost or the experience of a road trip.

By bus

Bus services can be convenient for travellers who do not want to drive. Check the latest operator, boarding point, departure time and arrival point before booking because schedules and routes can change.

By train

There is no direct railway station in Ooty for long-distance trains from Bengaluru. A common rail plan is to travel towards Mettupalayam or Coimbatore and continue to Ooty by road. If you specifically want to experience the Nilgiri Mountain Railway, check the current train schedule and availability before planning the rest of your journey.

By flight

Ooty does not have a commercial airport. Travellers can fly to a nearby airport such as Coimbatore and continue by road. This can make sense when flight availability is good, but the final road journey still needs to be included in your overall travel time.

Fuel Cost for Bangalore to Ooty

Fuel cost depends on your actual road distance, vehicle mileage and the current fuel price. Instead of relying on a generic trip-cost figure, calculate it using your own vehicle.

One-way fuel cost = Distance ÷ Mileage × Fuel price per litre

For example, if your trip is 280 km, your car delivers 15 km per litre and fuel costs ₹100 per litre, the estimated one-way fuel cost is about ₹1,867. Your actual cost can be higher or lower depending on traffic, driving conditions, detours and vehicle load.

For a complete budget, add tolls, parking, food, accommodation and local sightseeing. You can use our fuel cost calculator to estimate the fuel portion of your road-trip budget.

Best Time to Start from Bangalore

For a same-day drive, an early-morning departure is generally easier to manage than starting late. It gives you more daylight for the forest and hill sections and leaves room for breaks without pushing the journey into the evening.

Bandipur is an important planning point because the forest corridor has a night-traffic restriction. Current travel sources consistently report the restriction as approximately 9 PM to 6 AM; because operating rules can change, check the latest local or official information before departure.

For weekend and holiday travel, leave additional buffer for Bengaluru traffic and congestion near popular tourist areas. The objective should be to reach the hill section with enough daylight and without rushing.

Bangalore to Ooty Road Trip Tips

  • Start early: An early departure gives you more flexibility later in the journey.
  • Check the route before leaving: Compare the live navigation route rather than relying on an old screenshot or fixed distance.
  • Fuel up in advance: Do not enter a long forest or hill stretch with a low fuel level.
  • Respect the forest: Do not stop unnecessarily, feed animals, honk excessively or litter.
  • Drive patiently on ghats: Use a safe speed and allow extra distance around bends.
  • Carry essentials: Keep water, medicines, charging cables, vehicle documents and a basic emergency kit accessible.
  • Do not overpack the itinerary: Ooty is better enjoyed when you leave some time for traffic and unplanned stops.
  • Check current restrictions: Forest access, road conditions, permits and attraction timings can change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Ooty from Bangalore by road?

Ooty is approximately 270–290 km from Bengaluru by road, depending on your starting point and the route selected.

How many hours does it take from Bangalore to Ooty by car?

Plan for around 6–8 hours in normal conditions. Traffic, food breaks, road conditions, weather and the hill section can increase the journey time.

What is the best route from Bangalore to Ooty?

The commonly used route is Bengaluru → Mysuru → Gundlupet → Bandipur → Gudalur → Ooty. Your navigation app may suggest variations depending on live traffic and road conditions.

Is the Bangalore to Ooty route suitable for a road trip?

Yes. The journey combines highway driving, forest roads and Nilgiri hill roads. Drivers should be comfortable with winding roads and should allow extra time for the final mountain section.

Is Bandipur open at night?

The Bandipur forest corridor has a night-traffic restriction, commonly reported as 9 PM to 6 AM. Check current official or local travel information before your journey because restrictions can change.

How much fuel is required from Bangalore to Ooty?

Fuel required depends on your vehicle's mileage. Divide the one-way distance by your mileage to estimate litres, then multiply by the current fuel price.

Can I travel from Bangalore to Ooty by train?

There is no direct long-distance railway connection to Ooty. A practical option is to travel towards Mettupalayam or Coimbatore and continue by road, depending on the train schedule.

What should I budget for besides fuel?

Include tolls, food, parking, accommodation and local transport or sightseeing. These costs vary widely by vehicle, travel style and season.

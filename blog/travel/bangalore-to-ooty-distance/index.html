Bangalore to Ooty Distance, Route & Travel Time

Plan a Bangalore to Ooty road trip with route guidance, approximate travel time, stops and fuel-budget considerations.

Planning a trip from Bangalore to Ooty? The road journey is roughly 280–290 km, and the most common drive runs through Mysuru, Gundlupet, Bandipur and Gudalur before climbing into the Nilgiris. Actual distance and travel time vary with your starting point, route, traffic, stops and road conditions. This guide covers the Bangalore to Ooty distance, best road routes, expected travel time, fuel-cost calculation, useful stops and practical tips for planning the journey.

Bangalore to Ooty Distance at a Glance

The Bangalore to Ooty distance by road is approximately 280–290 km. The exact figure can change because Bengaluru is a large city, Ooty covers a wider hill-station area, and navigation apps may choose different road segments. Government tourism planning documents for the Nilgiris place Bangalore at around 290 km from Ooty, so it is useful to treat the distance as an approximate planning figure rather than a fixed number.

Bangalore to Ooty Route by Road

The most practical route for a Bangalore to Ooty road trip is through Mysuru, Gundlupet, Bandipur and Gudalur. It connects Bengaluru with the Nilgiris through a well-known South Indian road-trip corridor and gives travellers the option to stop at Mysuru or the forest areas along the way.

Common Bangalore to Ooty route

Bengaluru → Ramanagara/Channapatna side → Mysuru → Nanjangud → Gundlupet → Bandipur → Mudumalai → Gudalur → Ooty

The exact navigation route can vary depending on your starting point and live road conditions. Mysuru is a useful midpoint for many travellers because it is well connected by road and sits naturally on the journey towards Bandipur.

Alternative route through Masinagudi

Some travellers consider the Masinagudi and Kalhatty side when planning a Bangalore–Ooty drive. This route includes steep hill sections and hairpin bends, and road-use restrictions can apply to certain vehicles or directions. Check current local restrictions before choosing this option rather than assuming that every vehicle can use the same road.

Bangalore to Ooty Travel Time

Under normal conditions, the driving portion can take around 6–8 hours. That is a planning range, not a guaranteed arrival time. A road trip that starts during Bengaluru peak traffic can take longer before you even reach Mysuru.

For a comfortable itinerary, consider 7–9 hours as a realistic overall travel window after allowing for fuel, breakfast or lunch, restroom breaks, traffic and short sightseeing stops. If you are travelling with children, older family members or only one driver, building in additional buffer is sensible.

Best Route: Bengaluru → Mysuru → Bandipur → Gudalur → Ooty

For travellers looking for a straightforward road-trip plan, the Mysuru–Bandipur–Gudalur corridor is the key route to understand. Karnataka Tourism identifies Bandipur as being on the Mysuru–Ooty highway, while the Government of India’s Nilgiris destination planning document lists the Gudalur Ghats route as Gundlupet → Bandipur → Mudumalai → Gudalur → Ooty.

1. Bengaluru to Mysuru

This is the first major leg. Depending on where you start in Bengaluru and the time of day, traffic can make a noticeable difference. Starting early can help you avoid spending a large part of the trip in city traffic.

2. Mysuru to Gundlupet

After Mysuru, the route continues towards Gundlupet. This section is useful for a fuel or meal break if you want to enter the forest stretch with enough fuel and supplies.

3. Gundlupet to Bandipur

Bandipur is a major wildlife area on the Mysuru–Ooty corridor. Karnataka Tourism notes that Bandipur is part of the Nilgiri Biosphere Reserve and lies on the Mysuru–Ooty highway. Drivers should treat this section as a forest road rather than simply another highway segment.

4. Bandipur to Mudumalai and Gudalur

After Bandipur, the journey continues through the adjoining Nilgiri landscape and towards Gudalur. This is where the scenery changes noticeably from the plains and forest corridor to the hill environment around the Nilgiris.

5. Gudalur to Ooty

The final stretch is a hill-road climb towards Ooty. Travel time can vary considerably depending on traffic, weather and road conditions, so avoid planning your arrival around a very tight sightseeing reservation.

Important Bandipur Night Travel Restriction

If your Bangalore to Ooty road trip passes through Bandipur, timing matters. Current Karnataka Tourism information states that the highway through the reserve is closed to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM. This means a late-evening departure from Bengaluru can create an avoidable timing problem if you reach the forest corridor after the closure begins.

A better planning approach is to leave Bengaluru early enough to cross the relevant forest section within the permitted travel window. Always verify current restrictions before departure because forest-road rules can change.

Road-trip tip: Do not treat the shortest navigation estimate as your entire journey plan. Keep a time buffer for Bengaluru traffic, meals, fuel, forest-road restrictions and hill-road conditions.

Places and Stops on the Way

You do not need to turn the drive into a sightseeing marathon. A few planned stops can make the journey more comfortable without pushing your arrival time too late.

Mysuru

Mysuru is the most obvious major-city stop on the route. If you are starting early, it can work as a breakfast or coffee stop. If you are planning a slower two-day trip, Mysuru can also be used as an overnight base before continuing towards Ooty.

Bandipur

Bandipur is better treated as a wildlife corridor than a casual roadside stop. If you specifically want a safari experience, plan it separately and check the current booking and operating information before travelling.

Gudalur

Gudalur is useful as a practical break before the final hill stretch. Depending on your fuel, food and rest needs, it can be a sensible point to pause before continuing towards Ooty.

Pykara and other Ooty-area attractions

Once you reach the Nilgiris, attractions such as Pykara, Ooty Lake, the Botanical Garden and Doddabetta can be added to your itinerary. The Udhagamandalam Municipality lists these among the area's tourist attractions. Avoid adding too many stops on the same day if your priority is a relaxed trip.

Bangalore to Ooty by Bus, Train and Flight

A road trip is not the only way to travel between Bangalore and Ooty. Your choice depends on whether you value flexibility, lower driving effort or a faster long-distance connection.

By bus

Bus services connect Ooty with Bengaluru and other cities. The Nilgiris destination planning document also notes bus connectivity between Ooty and Bengaluru, Mysuru and nearby destinations. Check the current operator, boarding point, departure time and arrival point before booking.

By train

Ooty does not have a conventional long-distance railway connection directly from Bengaluru. Travellers commonly use a nearby railhead such as Mettupalayam and continue by road or the Nilgiri Mountain Railway, depending on the itinerary and available services.

By flight

Ooty has no commercial airport. Coimbatore is the closest major airport according to the Nilgiris destination planning document, while Bengaluru can be useful for travellers arriving from other cities and then continuing by road.

Bangalore to Ooty Fuel Cost Calculation

Your fuel cost depends on the distance, your vehicle's actual mileage and the current fuel price. A simple calculation gives you a useful starting estimate.

Fuel cost = Total distance ÷ Vehicle mileage × Fuel price per litre

For example, if your round-trip distance is approximately 580 km and your car delivers 15 km per litre, the trip would require about 38.7 litres of fuel. Multiply that figure by the current price per litre in your refuelling locations to estimate fuel spend.

For a more complete road-trip budget, add tolls, parking, food, accommodation and local sightseeing costs. You can use the CalcuPortal fuel cost calculator for the fuel portion of the estimate.

One-Day vs Two-Day Bangalore to Ooty Trip

Whether you should drive straight to Ooty or split the journey depends on your group and itinerary.

One-day trip

A one-day drive is possible when the main objective is to reach Ooty on the same day. Start early, keep the number of stops under control and avoid scheduling activities immediately after your expected arrival. A second driver can also make a long road journey easier to manage.

Two-day trip

A two-day plan gives you more flexibility. You can spend time in Mysuru or another suitable stop, rest properly and continue to Ooty the following day. This can be particularly useful for families or travellers who want to combine the drive with sightseeing.

Best Time for a Bangalore to Ooty Road Trip

Ooty can be visited across different seasons, but the road-trip experience changes with weather, traffic and holiday demand. Summer and festival periods can be busier, while monsoon conditions may require more cautious driving on hill roads.

If your priority is a comfortable road journey, check the weather forecast shortly before departure rather than relying only on a general seasonal rule. For detailed destination planning, see our best time to visit Ooty guide.

What to Pack for the Bangalore to Ooty Drive

  • Light layers or a jacket for the cooler hill climate.
  • Water and simple snacks for the journey.
  • Phone charger and a reliable power bank.
  • Driving licence, vehicle documents and required identification.
  • Basic medicines and personal essentials.
  • Comfortable footwear if you plan to walk around viewpoints and attractions.
  • Offline access to important booking details in case of weak mobile connectivity.

Bangalore to Ooty Road Trip Checklist

  • Check the live route and traffic before leaving Bengaluru.
  • Check weather conditions for both the plains and Nilgiris.
  • Start early enough to avoid Bengaluru peak traffic and forest-road timing issues.
  • Refuel before entering long stretches where fuel stations may be less convenient.
  • Take regular breaks instead of driving continuously for several hours.
  • Do not speed on forest or ghat sections.
  • Verify current Bandipur travel restrictions before departure.
  • Keep a buffer between your expected arrival and any prepaid activity.
  • Use current official attraction information for opening times, entry rules and ticket prices.

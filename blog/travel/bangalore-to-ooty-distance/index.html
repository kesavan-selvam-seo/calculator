Bangalore to Ooty Distance, Route & Travel Time

Distance, route options, drive time, Bandipur restrictions, stops and practical road-trip planning from Bangalore to Ooty.

Planning a Bangalore to Ooty trip by road? The journey is roughly 270–290 km, depending on where you start in Bangalore, the route you choose and the exact location in Ooty where you finish. Most travellers allow around 6–8 hours for the drive before adding longer meal, sightseeing or traffic delays.

The popular road journey takes you from Bangalore through Mysuru and Gundlupet, across the Bandipur forest corridor and into the Nilgiris. The final mountain section is slower than the highway portion, so planning the departure time, forest crossing and breaks is more useful than relying only on the shortest navigation estimate.

Bangalore to Ooty Distance at a Glance

Best Routes from Bangalore to Ooty

Route 1: Bangalore → Mysuru → Gundlupet → Bandipur → Gudalur → Ooty

This is the most straightforward route for travellers who want to use the Mysuru–Bandipur corridor. From Bangalore, the journey heads towards Mysuru before continuing through Nanjangud and Gundlupet. The road then enters the Bandipur forest corridor, continues towards Mudumalai and Gudalur, and finally climbs into Ooty.

Route 2: Bangalore → Mysuru → Gundlupet → Bandipur → Masinagudi → Ooty

The Masinagudi side is attractive for travellers who want the wildlife and hill-road experience around Mudumalai. However, the Sigur/Kalhatty side has steep terrain and route restrictions can apply to particular stretches and directions. If you are unfamiliar with the Nilgiris roads, use the route shown by current navigation and check local road conditions before choosing a shortcut.

Route 3: Bangalore → Salem → Mettupalayam → Coonoor → Ooty

The Salem–Coimbatore–Mettupalayam side is a useful alternative when you want to approach Ooty through Coonoor rather than the Bandipur corridor. It can make sense depending on traffic, your starting point in Bangalore and the rest of your itinerary. Compare the live route on the day of travel instead of assuming that one route will always be faster.

Bangalore to Ooty Travel Time

A normal road journey can take around 6–8 hours, but the actual time depends heavily on departure traffic and how long you spend at stops. The first part of the trip is faster highway travel, while the forest and Nilgiri sections require slower, more careful driving.

Which Route Should You Choose?

For a first road trip from Bangalore to Ooty, the Mysuru–Gundlupet–Bandipur–Gudalur corridor is a practical choice because it combines a major highway section with the established Karnataka–Nilgiris access route. Travellers interested in Coonoor can consider the Salem–Mettupalayam side, while experienced hill drivers may compare the Masinagudi side after checking current restrictions.

Bandipur Night Traffic Restriction

The Bandipur forest highway has a night traffic restriction. Karnataka Tourism currently describes the highway through the park as closed to traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM. This matters because a late departure from Bangalore can turn a simple road trip into a timing problem near the forest section.

Do not plan to reach the forest gate at the last possible minute. Traffic, breaks and weather can change the arrival time. Start early enough to leave a comfortable buffer and check the latest local instructions before travelling.

Important: Forest and road restrictions can be revised. Treat 9 PM–6 AM as the current planning reference, but verify the latest rule before your journey.

Best Stops on the Way from Bangalore to Ooty

Mysuru

Mysuru is the most natural major break on this route. You can use it for breakfast, lunch, fuel or a short sightseeing stop. If your goal is to reach Ooty early, keep the stop brief. If you are making the journey part of the holiday, Mysuru can easily become an overnight stop.

Nanjangud

Nanjangud is another possible stop after Mysuru. It works better for travellers who want a short break rather than a long sightseeing session. Your exact route may vary depending on navigation and traffic.

Gundlupet

Gundlupet is useful for checking fuel, taking a break and preparing for the forest stretch. Avoid entering the next section without enough fuel and water for your planned drive.

Bandipur

The Bandipur stretch is primarily a transit and wildlife corridor rather than a place to rush through. Follow speed restrictions, avoid unnecessary stops on the road and stay alert for animals and other vehicles.

Gudalur and the Nilgiris

Gudalur marks the transition into the hill-country section towards Ooty. From here, driving conditions become more winding and the journey can feel much slower than the earlier highway sections.

Bangalore to Ooty by Bus, Train and Flight

By Car

Driving is the most flexible option because you can choose your departure time, stop at Mysuru or other places and continue to Ooty without changing vehicles. Self-drive travellers should be comfortable with forest roads and mountain bends.

By Bus

Direct and connecting bus services can be convenient for travellers who do not want to drive. Journey duration varies by operator, route and traffic. Check the current service, boarding point, drop point and arrival time before booking.

By Train

Ooty does not have a broad-gauge railway station connected directly to Bangalore. A common plan is to travel by train towards Mettupalayam or Coimbatore and continue by road. The Nilgiri Mountain Railway can also be part of the experience from Mettupalayam, subject to current schedules and ticket availability.

By Flight

For a faster long-distance connection, travellers can fly from Bangalore to Coimbatore and continue to Ooty by road. This can be useful when time is more important than taking the entire Bangalore–Ooty road route.

Fuel Cost for Bangalore to Ooty

Fuel cost depends on the actual distance, vehicle mileage and current fuel price. Because fuel prices change, it is better to calculate the cost using the price you will actually pay rather than relying on a fixed online estimate.

One-way fuel cost = distance ÷ mileage × fuel price per litre

For example, if your route is 280 km, your car gives 14 km/l and fuel costs ₹100 per litre, the estimated fuel cost is 280 ÷ 14 × 100 = ₹2,000 for the one-way drive. This is only a calculation example; use your current fuel price and real-world mileage for your trip.

For a complete road-trip budget, also allow for tolls, parking, food and local sightseeing. If you are returning to Bangalore by car, calculate fuel for the full round trip rather than only the outbound journey.

One-Day vs Two-Day Road Trip

One-Day Bangalore to Ooty Drive

A one-way Bangalore to Ooty drive can fit into a single day if you start early and keep stops controlled. This works well when Ooty is the destination for a longer stay. A same-day return to Bangalore, however, creates a much longer driving day and leaves little time for sightseeing.

Two-Day Plan

A two-day road trip gives you more flexibility. You can start early from Bangalore, stop around Mysuru or another point, reach Ooty with enough daylight and spend the next day exploring. This is a more relaxed format for families, groups and travellers who prefer not to rush through the ghats.

Three-Day Plan

With three days, you can treat the road journey as part of the holiday rather than just a transfer. Spend time in Ooty, add Coonoor or nearby attractions, and keep the return journey separate from the main sightseeing day.

4+ daysMore time for Nilgiris sightseeing and a slower road-trip pace.

Best Time to Travel from Bangalore to Ooty

Ooty can be visited throughout the year, but road conditions and crowd levels vary by season. Summer and holiday periods can bring heavier tourist traffic, while monsoon travel may involve rain, mist and slower hill driving.

For a road trip, the most important consideration is not simply the weather at Ooty. Check the forecast for Bangalore, Mysuru, Bandipur and the Nilgiris, because conditions can change significantly along the route.

  • Summer: Popular for hill-station trips and school holidays; expect more traffic around major attractions.
  • Monsoon: Green landscapes can be attractive, but rain and mist require extra caution on the ghats.
  • Post-monsoon and winter: Often comfortable for sightseeing, although weekends and holiday periods can still be busy.

Practical Tips for the Bangalore to Ooty Drive

  1. Start early: An early departure gives you more daylight for the forest and ghat sections and leaves a buffer for traffic.
  2. Do not rush through Bandipur: Follow the applicable speed limits and current forest rules.
  3. Keep the final climb slow: The ghat section is not the place to maintain highway speeds.
  4. Plan fuel stops: Refuel before the longer forest and hill sections if your tank level requires it.
  5. Use live navigation: Check road closures, congestion and route changes shortly before departure.
  6. Allow extra time: A six-hour navigation estimate should not be treated as a guaranteed arrival time.
  7. Check current rules: Forest restrictions, permits, attraction timings and local traffic arrangements can change.
  8. Take breaks: Driver fatigue becomes especially important on a long highway-to-ghat journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Bangalore to Ooty distance by road?

The road distance is roughly 270–290 km, depending on your starting point, destination and route.

How long does it take to travel from Bangalore to Ooty by car?

A typical drive takes around 6–8 hours before allowing for longer sightseeing, meal or traffic delays.

What is the best route from Bangalore to Ooty?

A common route is Bangalore → Mysuru → Gundlupet → Bandipur → Mudumalai/Gudalur → Ooty.

Can I drive through Bandipur at night?

The Bandipur forest highway stretch has a night traffic restriction, commonly from 9 PM to 6 AM. Check current local rules before travelling.

Is there a direct train from Bangalore to Ooty?

There is no direct railway line into Ooty. Travellers commonly connect by rail to Mettupalayam or Coimbatore and continue by road or the Nilgiri Mountain Railway where suitable.

How do I calculate fuel cost for Bangalore to Ooty?

Divide the distance by your vehicle's mileage and multiply the result by the current fuel price per litre. Add tolls, parking and local travel for a fuller budget.

Which places can I stop at on the way to Ooty from Bangalore?

Mysuru, Nanjangud, Gundlupet, Bandipur and the Mudumalai/Gudalur area are common stop points, depending on your route and available time.

Is Bangalore to Ooty suitable for a one-day road trip?

It is possible to reach Ooty in a day, but a same-day return would leave limited time for sightseeing and involve a long drive. A 2-day or longer plan is more practical for most travellers.

What should I carry for a Bangalore to Ooty road trip?

Carry a valid driving licence and vehicle documents, medicines, chargers, water, light warm clothing and rain protection, and keep extra time for the ghat section.

When should I leave Bangalore for Ooty?

An early morning departure can help you make progress through the city and reach the forest and hill sections with more daylight. Choose departure time based on traffic, stops and current forest restrictions.

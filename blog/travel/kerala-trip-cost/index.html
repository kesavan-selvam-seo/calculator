Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Kerala trip cost?

There is no single fixed cost. Transport, accommodation, food, local travel, sightseeing and trip duration determine the final budget.

How can I reduce the trip cost?

Compare total transport costs, choose accommodation strategically, travel on suitable weekdays and group nearby attractions.

Should I keep an emergency buffer?

Yes. A separate buffer helps cover unexpected transport, weather-related changes or other travel expenses.

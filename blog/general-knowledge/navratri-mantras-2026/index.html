Navratri Mantras 2026: 9 Devi Mantras with Meaning

Updated October 17, 2026

Sharad Navratri honours the nine forms of Maa Durga, collectively known as the Navadurga. This guide brings together the 9 Navratri mantras for 2026 in Sanskrit, with simple transliteration, plain-English meaning and the day-wise Goddess sequence.

How to use this guide

Read the Sanskrit mantra, use the transliteration as a pronunciation aid, and follow the worship practice of your family or tradition. For exact ritual timings or pronunciation, refer to a trusted local Panchang, priest or traditional recitation.

For the commonly followed 2026 sequence, Sharad Navratri begins on Sunday, October 11. The nine Navadurga days run through Monday, October 19, followed by Vijayadashami on Tuesday, October 20. Lunar-tithi timings and some regional observances can vary by location.

Day 1: Maa Shailaputri Mantra

Day 1 · Oct 11 · Pratipada

Maa Shailaputri

ॐ देवी शैलपुत्र्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Shailaputryai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Shailaputri, revered as the daughter of the mountains.

Day 2 · Oct 12 · Dwitiya

Maa Brahmacharini

ॐ देवी ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Brahmacharinyai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Brahmacharini, associated with devotion, discipline and spiritual resolve.

Day 3 · Oct 13 · Tritiya

Maa Chandraghanta

ॐ देवी चन्द्रघण्टायै नमः॥

Om Devi Chandraghantayai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Chandraghanta, whose name refers to the crescent-moon bell associated with her form.

Day 4 · Oct 14 · Chaturthi

Maa Kushmanda

ॐ देवी कूष्माण्डायै नमः॥

Om Devi Kushmandayai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Kushmanda, traditionally associated with the cosmic creation and radiance.

Day 5 · Oct 15 · Panchami

Maa Skandamata

ॐ देवी स्कन्दमातायै नमः॥

Om Devi Skandamatayai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Skandamata, the mother of Skanda (Kartikeya).

Day 6 · Oct 16 · Shashthi

Maa Katyayani

ॐ देवी कात्यायन्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Katyayanyai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Katyayani, a revered form of Durga associated with courage and strength.

Day 7 · Oct 17 · Saptami

Maa Kalaratri

ॐ देवी कालरात्र्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Kalaratryai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Kalaratri, a fierce form revered as a protector against darkness and fear.

Day 8 · Oct 18 · Ashtami

Maa Mahagauri

ॐ देवी महागौर्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Mahagauryai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Mahagauri, whose form is associated with purity and serenity.

Day 9 · Oct 19 · Navami

Maa Siddhidatri

ॐ देवी सिद्धिदात्र्यै नमः॥

Om Devi Siddhidatryai Namah

Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Siddhidatri, whose name is associated with the bestowal of spiritual accomplishments or siddhis.

How to Chant Navratri Mantras

There is no single routine followed by every household. If it fits your tradition, you can sit in a clean, quiet place, take a moment to focus, read the mantra slowly and repeat it with attention. Some devotees recite the mantra associated with that day; others follow a family prayer, a stotra or guidance from a spiritual teacher.

Pronunciation note: Roman spellings are approximate. Sanskrit sounds can be represented in different ways in English, and regional recitation traditions may vary. If precise pronunciation matters to your practice, listen to a trusted traditional recitation or ask a knowledgeable teacher.

These are devotional name mantras and short explanations of their commonly understood meanings. This page does not promise specific outcomes from chanting; beliefs and spiritual experiences are personal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the nine Navratri mantras?

They are commonly recited name mantras for Maa Shailaputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri and Siddhidatri.

Can I chant a Navratri mantra without knowing Sanskrit?

You can use the transliteration as a starting aid. For careful pronunciation, listen to a trusted recitation or seek guidance from a knowledgeable teacher.

How many times should I chant a mantra?

Practices differ by family and tradition. Some people recite it once or a few times, while others follow a traditional count. Choose a practice that is comfortable and appropriate for you.

Which Goddess is worshipped on each Navratri day?

A commonly followed sequence begins with Shailaputri and continues through Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta, Kushmanda, Skandamata, Katyayani, Kalaratri, Mahagauri and Siddhidatri. Local customs may vary.

Are these mantras guaranteed to bring a specific result?

No specific outcome can be guaranteed. This guide shares devotional mantras and their commonly understood meanings; beliefs and experiences are personal.

Union Territories of India

India has 8 Union Territories: Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Lakshadweep and Puducherry. This guide covers their capitals, locations, administrative structure, formation and important GK facts.

A Union Territory (UT) is a territory of India that is administered under the constitutional framework for Union Territories. Unlike states, which have their own constitutional arrangement and elected state governments, the Union plays a more direct role in the administration of UTs. However, the exact structure is not identical for every UT.

What Is a Union Territory?

A Union Territory is a constituent administrative unit of India that comes under the Union's constitutional and administrative framework. Article 239 of the Constitution provides the general framework for administration of Union Territories through an Administrator appointed by the President, subject to the special provisions that apply to particular UTs.

This means the phrase "directly administered by the Centre" is useful as a simple GK explanation, but it should not be treated as meaning that every UT is administered in exactly the same way. Some UTs have legislatures and elected governments, while others do not.

Union Territories: Location and Key Facts

1. Andaman and Nicobar Islands

The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are an island group in the Bay of Bengal. The UT is geographically separated from mainland India and is known for its strategic location, tropical ecosystems and marine biodiversity. Its capital is Sri Vijaya Puram, the city formerly known as Port Blair.

2. Chandigarh

Chandigarh is a Union Territory in northern India and is also the shared capital of Punjab and Haryana. It is known for its planned urban design and its role as an important administrative centre.

3. Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu

Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu is a Union Territory on India's western side. The present UT was created on 26 January 2020 by merging the former Union Territories of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. Daman is the capital.

4. Delhi

Delhi is officially the National Capital Territory of Delhi. It is India's national capital territory and has a legislative assembly. New Delhi is the capital and the principal seat of the Union government.

5. Jammu and Kashmir

Jammu and Kashmir became a Union Territory on 31 October 2019 under the Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019. It has a legislature. Srinagar serves as the summer capital and Jammu as the winter capital under the territory's administrative arrangement.

6. Ladakh

Ladakh became a separate Union Territory on 31 October 2019 when the former State of Jammu and Kashmir was reorganised. It comprises the Leh and Kargil districts under the 2019 reorganisation framework and does not have a legislative assembly. Leh is its capital.

7. Lakshadweep

Lakshadweep is India's smallest Union Territory by area and consists of islands in the Arabian Sea, off the southwestern coast of India. Kavaratti is the capital. Its island geography makes it distinct from mainland Union Territories.

8. Puducherry

Puducherry is a Union Territory in southern India with a legislative assembly. Its territory includes four geographically separated regions: Puducherry, Karaikal, Mahe and Yanam. Puducherry is the capital.

Which Union Territories Have a Legislature?

Not all Union Territories have legislative assemblies. For quick GK revision, remember the three UTs with legislatures:

Union TerritoryLegislatureQuick Note
DelhiYesNational Capital Territory with a Legislative Assembly
Jammu and KashmirYesUnion Territory formed under the 2019 reorganisation framework
PuducherryYesUnion Territory with a Legislative Assembly

The other five Union Territories—Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Ladakh and Lakshadweep—do not have legislative assemblies.

How the Current 8 Union Territories Were Formed

The present list of eight UTs reflects several changes in India's administrative structure. Two of the most important recent changes are the creation of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh as separate Union Territories in 2019 and the merger of Dadra and Nagar Haveli with Daman and Diu in 2020.

ChangeYearWhat Happened?
Jammu and Kashmir reorganisation2019The former State of Jammu and Kashmir was reorganised into the Union Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.
Merger of two western UTs2020Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu became a single Union Territory.

The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 specifically provides for the formation of Ladakh as a Union Territory without a legislature and Jammu and Kashmir as a Union Territory with a legislature.

States vs Union Territories

The basic difference is the constitutional and administrative framework under which they are governed. States generally have their own elected state governments and legislatures under the Constitution, while Union Territories are administered under the Union Territories framework. The arrangement varies among UTs because Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir and Puducherry have legislatures.

How to Prepare Union Territories for GK Exams

For quick revision, do not try to memorise the topic as one long list. Start with the eight UT names and capitals, then learn the three UTs with legislatures. After that, revise the two major recent administrative changes: the 2019 Jammu and Kashmir reorganisation and the 2020 merger of Dadra and Nagar Haveli with Daman and Diu.

  • Memorise all 8 UTs with their capitals.
  • Mark island UTs separately: Andaman and Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep.
  • Remember the three UTs with legislatures using D-J-P.
  • Revise the 2019 and 2020 administrative changes.
  • Use a blank map of India to connect each UT with its location.

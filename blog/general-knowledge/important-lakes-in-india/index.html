Important Lakes in India

India has freshwater lakes, saltwater lakes, high-altitude lakes, lagoons and reservoirs.

Overview

Wular Lake in Jammu and Kashmir is an important freshwater lake, while Dal Lake in Srinagar is well known for its landscape and tourism.

Important Facts

Sambhar Lake in Rajasthan is a major inland salt lake. Chilika Lake in Odisha is a large brackish-water lagoon.

How to Study This Topic

Loktak Lake in Manipur is known for floating phumdis, while Vembanad Lake in Kerala is associated with the backwater system.

Quick Revision Guide

For revision, group lakes by type and location. This makes the list easier to remember.

Why This Topic Matters

Lake size, water level and ecological conditions can change, so current environmental status should be checked from official sources.

Quick revision tip: Group related facts together and verify current or changeable information from an official source when it matters for an examination.

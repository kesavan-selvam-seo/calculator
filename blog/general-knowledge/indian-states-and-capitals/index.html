Indian States and Capitals

Check all 28 Indian states and their capitals with a clear state-wise list, regional grouping and quick GK facts.

India has 28 states, and each state has a capital that serves as an important administrative centre. Learning the state-capital pairs is a basic but high-frequency topic in Indian geography, school quizzes, general awareness and competitive-exam preparation.

This guide gives the 28 states of India and their capitals in a simple table, followed by regional groupings, important facts, revision tips and frequently asked questions. India also has 8 Union Territories; they are covered separately so that state and Union Territory lists are not mixed.

Important Capital-Related Facts

Chandigarh is the capital of two states

Chandigarh serves as the capital of both Haryana and Punjab. It is also a Union Territory, making it a frequent general-knowledge question.

Amaravati and Andhra Pradesh

Amaravati is the capital associated with Andhra Pradesh in the current state-capital list. Capital-related developments have changed over time, so older study material can contain different arrangements. For current examination preparation, use the latest official Andhra Pradesh government information.

Dehradun and Uttarakhand

Dehradun is commonly listed as the capital of Uttarakhand. Uttarakhand also has Gairsain (Bhararisain) as the summer capital under state government arrangements. If an exam asks specifically for the state capital in a standard one-answer format, check the wording and prescribed source.

Shimla and Himachal Pradesh

Shimla is the commonly listed capital of Himachal Pradesh. The state has also used Dharamshala as a second capital under state government notification, so questions about “capital” and “second capital” should be read carefully.

States vs Union Territories

States and Union Territories are both parts of the Indian Union, but their constitutional and administrative arrangements differ. India currently has 28 states and 8 Union Territories. The National Portal of India lists the 8 Union Territories as Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Delhi, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Lakshadweep and Puducherry.

How to Memorise Indian States and Capitals

The easiest approach is to learn capitals in regional groups instead of memorising all 28 pairs at once.

  1. Start with the five southern states and their capitals.
  2. Learn the eight northeastern states as one group.
  3. Revise North, East, West and Central India separately.
  4. Remember special cases such as Chandigarh being shared by Haryana and Punjab.
  5. Use a blank India map to connect each state with its capital.
  6. Revise the 28-state table regularly and check official sources when a capital arrangement is subject to a recent administrative change.

Highest Mountains and Peaks in India

India's mountain geography includes the Himalayas, Karakoram and several important ranges across the peninsula and northeast.

Overview

Kangchenjunga is widely recognised as India's highest mountain peak and is located on the India-Nepal border. Nanda Devi is the highest peak located entirely within India.

Important Facts

Other important peaks and mountain regions include Kamet, Saltoro Kangri and the high mountains of Ladakh and the Himalayas.

How to Study This Topic

The Himalayas influence climate, river systems, biodiversity and human settlement. The Western Ghats and Eastern Ghats are also important mountain systems.

Quick Revision Guide

For exams, read the wording carefully because 'highest peak in India' and 'highest peak entirely within India' can lead to different answers depending on the convention used.

Why This Topic Matters

A map-based approach helps connect peaks with mountain ranges and neighbouring regions.

Quick revision tip: Group related facts together and verify current or changeable information from an official source when it matters for an examination.

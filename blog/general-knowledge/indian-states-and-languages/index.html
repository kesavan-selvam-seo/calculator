Indian States and Major Languages

India has exceptional linguistic diversity. This guide explains how to study state-language associations while remembering that many states are multilingual.

Overview

Language is an important part of India's cultural geography, and a state can have several languages spoken by different communities.

Important Facts

The Constitution's Eighth Schedule recognises 22 languages, including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada and Malayalam.

How to Study This Topic

Language and script are different concepts, so learning both can make language-related GK easier.

Quick Revision Guide

For revision, learn the major regional language commonly associated with a state while remembering that language use does not stop at state borders.

Why This Topic Matters

This topic is useful for school assignments, quizzes and cultural GK preparation.

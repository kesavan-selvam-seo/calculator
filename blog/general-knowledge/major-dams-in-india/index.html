Major Dams in India

India's major dams support irrigation, hydropower, drinking-water supply, flood management and regional water needs. For GK preparation, the easiest way to remember them is by connecting each dam with its river and state.

Major dams in India are important for geography, general knowledge and competitive-exam preparation. Some are closely associated with major river-valley projects, while others are important for irrigation, water supply or hydropower. This guide covers 20+ well-known dams with their rivers, states and key facts.

Important Dams You Should Know

1. Bhakra Dam

Bhakra Dam is built on the Sutlej River in Himachal Pradesh and is part of the wider Bhakra-Beas water-resource system. The project supports irrigation and power supply across northern India. The Bhakra-Beas Management Board regulates water and power from the Bhakra-Nangal and Beas projects for several beneficiary states and regions.

2. Tehri Dam

Tehri Dam is located on the Bhagirathi River in Uttarakhand. It is associated with hydropower generation, irrigation and water supply. Tehri is one of the most frequently asked dam-river-state combinations in Indian GK.

3. Sardar Sarovar Dam

Sardar Sarovar is built across the Narmada River in Gujarat. The project is important for irrigation and water supply and also has a major hydropower component. The Narmada basin's major hydropower projects include Sardar Sarovar, Indira Sagar and Omkareshwar.

4. Hirakud Dam

Hirakud Dam is built across the Mahanadi River at Sambalpur in Odisha. It is associated with flood moderation, irrigation and power generation and is one of India's best-known multipurpose river-valley projects.

5. Nagarjuna Sagar Dam

Nagarjuna Sagar is a major project on the Krishna River in the Telangana–Andhra Pradesh region. It is important for irrigation and hydropower and is a common question in geography and general-awareness examinations.

6. Mettur Dam

Mettur Dam is built on the Cauvery River in Tamil Nadu. The reservoir is important for irrigation and water supply in the Cauvery delta and adjoining areas, making Mettur a key dam-river association for South India GK.

7. Koyna Dam

Koyna Dam is located in Maharashtra on the Koyna River. The Koyna project is especially known for hydropower generation and is an important part of Maharashtra's water and power infrastructure.

Why Are Dams Built?

A dam can serve one or several purposes. Modern water-resource projects are often designed as multipurpose systems rather than for a single function.

PurposeHow Dams Help
IrrigationStores and regulates water for agriculture through canals and distribution systems.
HydropowerUses the movement or fall of stored/released water to generate electricity.
Drinking-water supplyProvides regulated water storage for cities, towns and communities.
Flood managementReservoir storage and controlled releases can help manage certain flood conditions.
Industrial waterCan provide a regulated source of water for industrial requirements.
Water storageHelps maintain supplies across seasons when inflows vary.

  3. Hirakud Dam is associated with the Mahanadi River and Odisha.
  4. Sardar Sarovar Dam is associated with the Narmada River and Gujarat.
  5. Nagarjuna Sagar Dam is associated with the Krishna River.

  7. Ukai Dam is associated with the Tapti/Tapi River in Gujarat.
  8. Rihand Dam is located in Uttar Pradesh on the Rihand River.

How to Prepare Major Dams in India for GK

Use a layered approach instead of memorising a long list randomly. First learn the most frequently asked dam-river-state pairs. Then group dams by river basin and finally revise their primary purposes.

  • Step 1: Memorise 10 core pairs such as Bhakra–Sutlej and Tehri–Bhagirathi.
  • Step 2: Add the state or region to each pair.
  • Step 3: Group dams under Narmada, Krishna, Cauvery, Chambal and other major rivers.
  • Step 4: Learn whether the project is mainly associated with irrigation, hydropower, water supply, flood management or multiple uses.
  • Step 5: Verify exact heights, capacities and current project details from official sources when needed.

Major Dams in India

Dams are important GK topics because they connect geography, rivers, agriculture, water management and power generation.

Overview

Examples include Bhakra Dam on the Sutlej, Tehri Dam on the Bhagirathi, Sardar Sarovar on the Narmada, Hirakud on the Mahanadi and Nagarjuna Sagar on the Krishna.

Important Facts

The purpose of a dam can include irrigation, hydropower, drinking-water supply, flood management or a combination of these functions.

How to Study This Topic

For GK preparation, use a three-part memory pattern: dam, river and state.

Quick Revision Guide

Technical figures such as height, reservoir capacity and power output should be checked with the relevant project authority if exact numbers are required.

Quick revision tip: Group related facts together and verify current or changeable information from an official source when it matters for an examination.

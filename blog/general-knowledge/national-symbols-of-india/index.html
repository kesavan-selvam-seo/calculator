National Symbols of India

India's national symbols represent different aspects of the country's identity and heritage.

Overview

The national flag is the Tricolour with saffron, white and green bands and the Ashoka Chakra at the centre. The State Emblem is adapted from the Lion Capital of Ashoka at Sarnath.

Important Facts

Jana Gana Mana is India's National Anthem, while Vande Mataram is the National Song.

How to Study This Topic

The national animal is the Bengal tiger, the national bird is the Indian peacock, the national flower is the lotus and the national tree is the banyan.

Quick Revision Guide

The Ganges river dolphin is recognised as India's National Aquatic Animal, while the mango is the national fruit.

Why This Topic Matters

For revision, separate India's national symbols from state-level symbols such as state animals, birds, flowers and trees.

Quick revision tip: Group related facts together and verify current or changeable information from an official source when it matters for an examination.

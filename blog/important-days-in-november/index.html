Important National Days in November 2026 – India

  • November 7 – National Cancer Awareness Day: observed in India to promote awareness about cancer prevention, early detection and treatment.
  • November 9 – National Legal Services Day: highlights access to free legal aid and awareness of legal services.
  • November 11 – National Education Day: commemorates the birth anniversary of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, India’s first Education Minister.
  • November 14 – Children’s Day: observed in India on the birth anniversary of Jawaharlal Nehru.
  • November 16 – National Press Day: recognises the importance of a free, responsible and ethical press.
  • November 26 – Constitution Day: commemorates the adoption of the Constitution of India on November 26, 1949.

International and UN Observances in November 2026

  • November 5 – World Tsunami Awareness Day: focuses on preparedness, early warning systems and reducing tsunami risk.
  • November 10 – World Science Day for Peace and Development: promotes the role of science in society and sustainable development.
  • November 14 – World Diabetes Day: raises awareness about diabetes prevention, diagnosis and management.
  • November 16 – International Day for Tolerance: encourages respect, inclusion and understanding across communities.
  • November 19 – World Toilet Day: draws attention to sanitation and access to safely managed toilets.
  • November 20 – World Children’s Day: promotes children’s rights and well-being worldwide.
  • November 21 – World Television Day: recognises television’s role in communication and public information.
  • November 25 – International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women: marks the start of the annual 16 Days of Activism against gender-based violence.
  • November 26 – World Sustainable Transport Day: highlights transport systems that support sustainable development.

Key November 2026 Days Explained

National Cancer Awareness Day – November 7

India observes National Cancer Awareness Day every November 7 to encourage awareness around prevention, screening and early detection. The date is linked with the birth anniversary of Marie Curie, whose scientific work contributed to the development of radiation-based cancer treatment.

Diwali – November 8

Diwali falls on Sunday, November 8, 2026. The festival is associated with light, prosperity and the victory of good over evil. Lakshmi Puja is observed on the same date in many parts of India.

National Education Day – November 11

National Education Day is observed every November 11 in honour of Maulana Abul Kalam Azad, the first Education Minister of independent India. The day highlights the role of education in national development and social progress.

Children’s Day in India – November 14

India celebrates Children’s Day on November 14, the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru. The same date is also recognised internationally as World Diabetes Day.

National Press Day – November 16

National Press Day marks the role of the Press Council of India and is used to reflect on press freedom, journalistic responsibility and ethical reporting.

Guru Nanak Jayanti – November 24

Guru Nanak Jayanti falls on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. It commemorates the birth of Guru Nanak, the founder of Sikhism and the first Sikh Guru. The date coincides with Kartik Purnima in 2026.

Constitution Day – November 26

Constitution Day, also known as Samvidhan Diwas, is observed on November 26 to commemorate the adoption of the Constitution of India by the Constituent Assembly in 1949. The Constitution came into effect on January 26, 1950.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the most important days in November 2026?

Key dates include World Tsunami Awareness Day on November 5, National Cancer Awareness Day on November 7, Diwali on November 8, National Education Day on November 11, Children’s Day on November 14, National Press Day on November 16, World Toilet Day on November 19, Guru Nanak Jayanti on November 24, and Constitution Day on November 26.

When is Diwali in 2026?

Diwali falls on Sunday, November 8, 2026.

When is Children’s Day in India in 2026?

Children’s Day falls on Saturday, November 14, 2026.

When is Guru Nanak Jayanti in 2026?

Guru Nanak Jayanti falls on Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

When is Constitution Day in 2026?

Constitution Day falls on Thursday, November 26, 2026.

Is World Children’s Day the same as Children’s Day in India?

No. India observes Children’s Day on November 14, while the United Nations observes World Children’s Day on November 20.
